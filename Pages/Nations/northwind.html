Northwind Empire

The largest nation of Drakma, located in the far north.

Nation's Informations

Capital City of Dorsek
Government Monarchy
Population 13 Million
Demonym Northlander
Continent Drakma

Overview

Among the nations of Drakma, Northwind is by far the largest one, but also the most isolated. Located north of the Crown of Fangs, the only large and relatively safe routes to enter the nation are through the Howling Canion and the western shores.

Like other nations Northwind can trace its founding to the Age of Heroes, being a bit younger than Heavenrock. It was founded by Boreas the Conqueror, who subjugated the neighboring tribes and warlords to and initiated the construction of the First Capital, Windrise, though centuries later the city would be destroyed and taken over by the dragon, Vaelkros, becoming known as Windfall.

Geography

Terrain

The territories of Northwind reach from one extremity of Drakma to the other, the climate is mostly cold throughout the year and most of its lands are comprised of tundras, fiords, mountainous regions, ancient forests, valleys and jagged shorelines. The Crown of Fangs can be seen from most of the kingdom, stretching from east to west, cutting the landscape.

Climate

Northwind experiences mostly cold climates due to its geography, during the winter, it snows almost across all of its territory, most harbors end up frozen during the winter. The regions furthest to the north remain covered in ice and snow almost constantly, blizzards and snow storms are pretty common during winter season in these regions.

Natural Resources

Due to its climate, Northwind isn't able to produce certain kind of crops, having to rely on hardy and cold resistant plants. Live stock may include species of sheep, cow, goats and even bison in some locations, aside from other native species. Northwind is also the second-largest exporter of mineral resources in the continent, only behind Garmorys, some of these minerals can only be found in Northwind and are very valuable.

History

Due to its isolation from the rest of the continent, most of Northwind's history are from the era before their unification and recollections of civil wars and conflicts that happened after it.

On the grand scheme of things, Northwind isn't very influential, they are certainly respected, especially by their close alies, but aside from its neighbors, the other nations barely interact with Northwind.

Population and Culture

The population of Heavenrock is quite simillar to the natives of Wyrmcrest and Varlon, sharing the same cultural roots with them. Heavenrock has always valued the arts and nobility far more than its neighbors, for this reason they neglected anything that seemed brutsh or barbaric to them, there are few prpper warriors in Heavenrock asside from the military.

The independence of the Abyss region came due to a large rift forming between them and the rest of the nation, mostly due to them choosing to maintain certain traditions and the growing differences between the culture of the north and south of the nation.

Economy

Most of the economy of Heavenrock is relaiant the magic items they produce and their crops, for most of their history this was enough to sustain the whole nation, though in recent years due to the depletion of many sources of magical materials and ingredients they have been forced to import them, increasing the costs of production drasticaly.

Their agricultural production on the other hand has been triving thanks to their use of golens for heavy labor, they trade their goods mostly with Wyrmcrest, Northwind, Varlon and Pelagia.

Government

Heavenrock is governed by a hereditary monarchy that has ruled since the kingdom's founding. The monarch is the defacto ruller of the nation, with other nobles trying to gain favor with them most of the time.

Over the centuries the Magic Academy gained a lot of power and influence within the nation, being the only other faction that can even consider challeging the crown.

Foreign Relations

Heavenrock tries to maintain positive relations with the neighboring nations, especialy Wyrmcrest, Varlon and Northwind. They also have remained somewhat friendly with the region of the Abyss after its separation from them, on the other hand Dragonfell is still considered their enemy, being the only nation Heavenrock tends to be actively hostile to.

Due to Dragonfell atempting to claim the Abyss for thenselves multiple times, Heavenrock has kept some of its military permanently position near the borders, despite the leaders of the Abyss claiming the can deffend themselves, Heavenrock has insisted in doing so as an "act of generosity" to them.
