Northwind Empire

The largest nation of Drakma, located in the far north.

Nation's Informations

Capital City of Dorsek
Government Monarchy
Population 13 Million
Demonym Northlander
Continent Drakma

Overview

Among the nations of Drakma, Northwind is by far the largest one, but also the most isolated. Located north of the Crown of Fangs, the only large and relatively safe routes to enter the nation are through the Howling Canion and the western shores.

Like other nations Northwind can trace its founding to the Age of Heroes, being a bit younger than Heavenrock. It was founded by Boreas the Conqueror, who subjugated the neighboring tribes and warlords to and initiated the construction of the First Capital, Windrise, though centuries later the city would be destroyed and taken over by the dragon, Vaelkros, becoming known as Windfall.

Despite its immense size, Northwind has rarely pursued territorial expansion beyond its own borders. The kingdom's rulers have long been occupied with governing its vast and often inhospitable lands, where harsh winters, great distances and isolated provinces make administration a constant challenge. Rather than projecting power abroad, Northwind has traditionally focused on maintaining internal stability and securing the few routes that connect it to the rest of Drakma.

Although considered politically distant by many southern nations, Northwind commands considerable respect due to its military strength, mineral wealth and centuries-old alliance with Wyrmcrest. Its soldiers are renowned for enduring conditions that few other armies could survive, while its mines produce valuable resources found nowhere else on the continent.

Geography

Terrain

Northwind possesses one of the largest and most diverse landscapes in Drakma. Towering mountain ranges, frozen coastlines, deep fjords, dense forests and endless tundras create natural barriers that have historically isolated entire regions from one another. Even today, traveling between provinces may require weeks of crossing mountain passes, rivers or frozen plains.

During winter, countless rivers and lakes freeze solid, creating temporary roads used by merchants, travelers and military patrols. Conversely, spring thaw often floods valleys and washes away roads, making seasonal travel unpredictable throughout much of the kingdom.

Climate

Northwind experiences mostly cold climates due to its geography, during the winter, it snows across all of its territory, most harbors end up frozen during the winter, blocking ships from docking. The regions furthest to the north remain covered in ice and snow almost constantly, blizzards and snow storms are pretty common during winter season in these regions.

Northwind's people have adapted their architecture and daily lives to endure the long winters. Homes are typically constructed with thick stone foundations and heavy timber walls, while steep roofs prevent dangerous snow accumulation. Nearly every settlement is centered around communal hearths, bathhouses or geothermal springs that provide warmth during the coldest months.

Natural Resources

Northwind's harsh climate severely limits agriculture. Only hardy, cold-resistant crops can survive the long winters, forcing the kingdom to rely on resilient farming techniques and extensive imports from its southern neighbors. Live stock may include species of sheep, cow, goats and even bison in some locations, aside from other native species.

Besides conventional ores such as iron, silver and copper, Northwind is home to several rare minerals that are highly sought after throughout Drakma. Many of these deposits are found deep beneath the Crown of Fangs or within the frozen highlands, making their extraction difficult but extremely profitable. These resources have fueled the kingdom's economy for centuries and remain one of its greatest strategic advantages.

Vast forests in the west also provide some of the finest timber on the continent. Trees from the Shrouded Taiga are prized for their remarkable durability and are commonly used in the construction of ships, fortresses and luxury furniture.

History

Throughout much of its history, Northwind struggled to maintain unity. Before Emperor Boreas, Northwind's territories were divided among dozens of small kingdoms and tribal factions, despite his efforts he was only able to unite the east during his lifetime. Its immense territory and rugged geography allowed regional rulers to retain significant independence, leading to numerous succession disputes and civil wars. Several dynasties rose and fell before the imperial authority finally established lasting control over the western provinces roughly three centuries ago.

The destruction of Windrise marked one of the darkest moments in Northwind's history. The loss of its capital not only displaced thousands of citizens but also shattered the kingdom's political center. The founding of Dorsek symbolized a new era of reconstruction, with the city becoming both the administrative and cultural heart of the empire.

Population and Culture

While united beneath a single crown, the people of Northwind possess a wide variety of regional identities. Eastern communities generally trace their ancestry to the original kingdoms founded during the Age of Heroes, whereas many western settlements preserve customs inherited from the independent realms that resisted imperial rule for centuries.

Hospitality is considered a cornerstone of Northwind culture. Travelers who survive the harsh northern wilderness are traditionally offered food, warmth and shelter before any questions are asked. Refusing hospitality to someone in genuine need is viewed as deeply dishonorable throughout much of the kingdom.

Storytelling also occupies an important role within Northwind society. Tales of legendary heroes, dragons and ancient kings are commonly shared during the long winter nights, helping preserve local history and traditions across generations.

Technically the region of the Wildlands is also considered part of Northwind's territories, though in reality they have done little to properly colonize it or explore those lands. The nomadic tribes that inhabit this vast and harsh region have been living in isolation for thousands of years and have developed their own language and culture, separated from the rest of Drakma.

Economy

Mining, forestry and animal husbandry form the backbone of Northwind's economy. Although agriculture remains limited by the climate, fishing, livestock and imported grain allow even the most remote settlements to survive. The kingdom also exports large quantities of furs, wool, timber, gemstones and worked metal throughout the continent.

Due to the seasonal freezing of many northern ports, trade is heavily concentrated during the warmer months. As a result, merchants often store large quantities of supplies before winter, while caravans through the Howling Canion become increasingly important whenever maritime trade becomes impossible.

Government

Although the Emperor possesses absolute authority under the law, provincial governors exercise considerable autonomy in local affairs. This decentralized system developed out of necessity, as the enormous distances separating Northwind's settlements make direct administration from the capital impractical.

Noble houses remain highly influential within imperial politics. Many have ruled the same territories for generations and maintain their own military forces to defend local borders and settlements. Their loyalty is essential for preserving stability throughout the empire.

Foreign Relations

Despite their isolation, Northwind has been able to keep close relations with nations like Wyrmcrest, Heavenrock and Falkore. They also have trading with Pelagia, Dragonfell and Garmorys, though that's as far as their relation goes.

In the case of Wyrmcrest, both nations have been close alies since the Age of Heroes. The only time they came into conflict was during a civil war in Wyrmcrest where Northwind supported the rebel side to remove the Usurper King. Due to Wyrmcrest controlling the Howling Canion, they are the only ones with a large and safe inland trade route with Northwind.

Relations with Heavenrock have grown considerably over recent decades. Both kingdoms recognize the economic benefits of stronger cooperation, particularly through the ambitious excavation of a tunnel beneath the Crown of Fangs. If completed, the project would establish the first reliable year-round trade route between the two nations.

  • The Frigid Sea — A large and treacherous stretch of sea that separates Northwind from the Wildlands. For most of the year the waters here are too turbulent to be safely crossed, and storm are quite frequent.
