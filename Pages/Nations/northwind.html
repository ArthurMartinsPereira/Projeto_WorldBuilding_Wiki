Northwind Empire

The largest nation of Drakma, located in the far north.

Nation's Informations

Capital City of Dorsek
Government Monarchy
Population 13 Million
Demonym Northlander
Continent Drakma

Overview

Among the nations of Drakma, Northwind is by far the largest one, but also the most isolated. Located north of the Crown of Fangs, the only large and relatively safe routes to enter the nation are through the Howling Canion and the western shores.

Like other nations Northwind can trace its founding to the Age of Heroes, being a bit younger than Heavenrock. It was founded by Boreas the Conqueror, who subjugated the neighboring tribes and warlords to and initiated the construction of the First Capital, Windrise, though centuries later the city would be destroyed and taken over by the dragon, Vaelkros, becoming known as Windfall.

Geography

Terrain

The territories of Northwind reach from one extremity of Drakma to the other, the climate is mostly cold throughout the year and most of its lands are comprised of tundras, fiords, mountainous regions, ancient forests, valleys and jagged shorelines. The Crown of Fangs can be seen from most of the kingdom, stretching from east to west, cutting the landscape.

Climate

Northwind experiences mostly cold climates due to its geography, during the winter, it snows almost across all of its territory, most harbors end up frozen during the winter. The regions furthest to the north remain covered in ice and snow almost constantly, blizzards and snow storms are pretty common during winter season in these regions.

Natural Resources

Due to its climate, Northwind isn't able to produce certain kind of crops, having to rely on hardy and cold resistant plants. Live stock may include species of sheep, cow, goats and even bison in some locations, aside from other native species. Northwind is also the second-largest exporter of mineral resources in the continent, only behind Garmorys, some of these minerals can only be found in Northwind and are very valuable.

History

Due to its isolation from the rest of the continent, most of Northwind's history is from things related to its founding, civil wars and other conflicts. Due to the size of their territory they are the nation that took the longest time to fully unify, that only happened around 320 years ago.

On the grand scheme of things, Northwind isn't very influential, they are certainly respected, especially by their close alies, like Wyrmcrest, Heavenrock and Falkore. But aside from its neighbors, the other nations barely interact with Northwind.

Population and Culture

The population and culture of Northwind is very varied, the dominant one depends on the region you are on. though the two big divisions are between the east and west of the nation, this is due to the west being the last region to unite with the rest. They used to be composed of many pety kingdoms until they were conquered a few centuries ago.

Technically the region of the Wildlands is considered part of Northwind's territories, though in reality they have done little to properly colonize it or explore those lands. The nomadic tribes that inhabit this vast and harsh region have been living in isolation for thousands of years and have developed their own language and culture, separated from the rest of Drakma.

Economy

Northwind's economy is driven mostly by their abundance in rare ores unique to their lands and a few local specialties. In ore and gem exports they are only behind Garmorys, they also extract a lot of timber from their vast eastern forest. This wood is highly valuable in the construction of ships,furniture and houses.

They import most of their food from Wyrmcrest through the Howling Canion, during the winter months most of their ports end up frozen. Recently Heavenrock has been trying to increase their trading with Northwind, even initiating the excavation of giant tunel through the Crown of Fangs, supposedly to create a new route for trading.

Government

Northwind is reled by the Imperial Family and the nobles from important houses. Due to their vast territories they were forced to divide the administration in various provinces.

Each province can elect their own ruler, but usually these are still from noble houses due to popularity or funding. Ultimately the Emperor or Empress has absolute power and is the head of the military.

Foreign Relations

Despite their isolation, Northwind has been able to keep close relations with nations like Wyrmcrest, Heavenrock and Falkore. They also have trading with Pelagia, Dragonfell and Garmorys, though that's as far as their relation goes.

In the case of Wyrmcrest, both nations have been close alies since the Age of Heroes. The only time they came into conflict was during a civil war in Wyrmcrest where Northwind supported the rebel side to remove the Usurper King. Due to Wyrmcrest controlling the Howling Canion, they are the only ones with a large and safe inland trade route with Northwind.
