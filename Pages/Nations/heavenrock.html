The birthplace of Path Magic, also known as the nation of magic.

Overview

The Kingdom of Heavenrock is located to the west of the Heartlands, being a neighbor of Wyrmcrest, Varlon and Dragonfell. To its north lays the Crown of Fangs, separating it from Northwind, and to the south lays the region of the Abyss. Heavenrock is also known as the Nation of Magic due to being the birthplace Path Magic and the Home of the Magic Academy. Magic is used in almost everything in Heavenrock, from farming and building to healing and entertaiment, they are especialy known for the use of golens for transport and hard labour.

The founding of Heavenrock happened close to the end of the Age of Heroes by king Arlan the Conqueror. He was able to conquer much of the territories that would compose Heavenrock one day through wars and aliances.

Geography

Terrain

The lands of Heavenrock are very varied, they include, deep forests, swamps, plains, rolling hills, mountainous regions, fiords, river valleys, islands and sandy beaches. The Abyss acts as a large natural barrier in the south, while the Dark Forest protects most of the east and the Crown of Fangs blocks the north, to the west lays the infamous Ashen Islands and the Smoldering Sea.

Climate

Heavenrock experiences mostly cold and temperate climates due to its geography. Northern regions, particularly close to the Crown of Fangs, receive regular snowfall during winter, while the southern territories enjoy milder weather thanks to their proximity to the coasts, the Abyss and the deserts of Dragonfell.

Natural Resources

Heavenrock is known for possessing abundant magical resources and fertile farmland, when it comes to regular mineral deposits they tend to lack large stable sorces. Due to their over reliance on magical resources, many of them are getting depleated at an alarming rate, forcing the nation to import more from its neighbors.

History

The kingdom traces its origins to the end of the Age of Heroes. According to historical records, King Arlan I united the region after many wars and aliances, establishing the Kingdom of Heavenrock, over the comming centuries more territories were slowly added, including the Abyss, though eventualy it became independent again.

Over the following centuries since its founding, Heavenrock's influence spread across the continent, mostly thanks to the Magic Academy's efforts'. Though after the creation and expancion of the Hunters Guild this influence has steadly diminished.

Population and Culture

The population of Heavenrock is quite simillar to the natives of Wyrmcrest and Varlon, sharing the same cultural roots with them. Heavenrock has always valued the arts and nobility far more than its neighbors, for this reason they neglected anything that seemed brutsh or barbaric to them, there are few prpper warriors in Heavenrock asside from the military.

The independence of the Abyss region came due to a large rift forming between them and the rest of the nation, mostly due to them choosing to maintain certain traditions and the growing differences between the culture of the north and south of the nation.

Economy

Most of the economy of Heavenrock is relaiant the magic items they produce and their crops, for most of their history this was enough to sustain the whole nation, though in recent years due to the depletion of many sources of magical materials and ingredients they have been forced to import them, increasing the costs of production drasticaly.

Their agricultural production on the other hand has been triving thanks to their use of golens for heavy labor, they trade their goods mostly with Wyrmcrest, Northwind, Varlon and Pelagia.

Government

Heavenrock is governed by a hereditary monarchy that has ruled since the kingdom's founding. The monarch is the defacto ruller of the nation, with other nobles trying to gain favor with them most of the time.

Over the centuries the Magic Academy gained a lot of power and influence within the nation, being the only other faction that can even consider challeging the crown.

Foreign Relations

Heavenrock tries to maintain positive relations with the neighboring nations, especialy Wyrmcrest, Varlon and Northwind. They also have remained somewhat friendly with the region of the Abyss after its separation from them, on the other hand Dragonfell is still considered their enemy, being the only nation Heavenrock tends to be actively hostile to.

Due to Dragonfell atempting to claim the Abyss for thenselves multiple times, Heavenrock has kept some of its military permanently position near the borders, despite the leaders of the Abyss claiming the can deffend themselves, Heavenrock has insisted in doing so as an "act of generosity" to them.

Notable Locations

Cities and Settlements

  • Heavenrock City — The capital of the kingdom, its located on the western coast of the Drakma, one of the main points of comerce in the west.
  • Lurimel City — A city located on an island at the center of the Azure Lake, famous for its enchanters and artificers.
  • Clementia City — A city located near the boder with the Abyss region, known for its musicians, artists, and sculptors.
  • Brightfang Town — An very old mining settlement located near the Crown of Fangs, it has been abandoned and rebuilt many times.
  • Silverhollow — A city once known for it large Lunar Silver mines, now most of them have runned out of ore and the city is in decline.
  • Astrelia Harbor — Also known as the City of Astrologers, its located in the region of the Cristaline Basin, the Grand Observatory is also located here.
  • Marely — Located in the region of the Ancestral Falls, Marely is the only large settlement around. It serves as a stop point before entering Northwind by sea.

Natural Regions

  • The Crown of Fangs — A massive mountain chain that cuts the continent, separating the great north from the rest.
  • Bone Marsh — A swampy region located near the Abyss and the Somber Forest, its filled with the bones of monsters and inimals.
  • The Somber Forest — A large forest that covers much of the eastern territories of the kingdom, known for its rare magical flora and fauna, there are also a few ruins hidden in there.
  • Turquoise Coast — Covering much of the western coastal region of the kingdom, known for its many beaches and beautiful scenary.
  • Starry Plains — Located near the heart of the kingdom, these plains gained their name thanks to the Star Lilies that bloom there almost all year around, the Crest Forge is located here.
  • Gilded Woods — A forest of trees with golden leaves, the sap, fruits and wood of these trees have certain magical atributes.
  • Windward Knolls — Located between the Somber Forest and the Crown of Fangs, this region is filled with many hills and a constant wind coming from the Crown of Fangs.
  • Crystaline Basin — A region dotted with lakes and crystal formations formed by centuries of magical saturation. Astrelia Harbor is located here.
  • Emberroot Flats — A region technically belonging to the Abyss, was abandoned long ago due to the same magical disaster that created the Molten Badlands.
  • Ancestral Falls — An large archipelago af many dozens of islands located between Northwind and Heavenrock. Known for its Oceanic Platforms and Chalice Islands.

Landmarks and Points of Interest

  • Shatered Plains — An old battlefield dating back to the War of The Three Crowns, the landscape is filled with reminants of the war, including the Living Fortress.
  • Magic Academy of Heavenrock — One of the most important and influential organizations if the continent, only behind the Hunters Guild.
  • Grayhall Ruins — The ruins of an old abandoned fortress that was used in the War of The Three Crowns by the rebels. Located in the Somber Forest
  • The Black Keep — Also known as Castle Valkor, this fortress has been abandoned since the War of The Three Crowns, its rumored to be haunted.
  • The Grand Observatory — Located in Astrelia Harbor, this is the main headquarters of the Astrologers Association.
  • The Crest Forge — An old almost forgotten site, in the past it was used to create Crested Enchantments, the place is still maintained by an order of guardians.
