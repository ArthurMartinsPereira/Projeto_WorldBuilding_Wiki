Kingdom of Wyrmcrest

The economic heart of Drakma and the continent's largest trading power.

Nation's Informations

Capital City of Wyrmcrest
Government Monarchy
Population 28 Million
Demonym Wyrmcrestian / Heartlander
Continent Drakma

Overview

The Kingdom of Wyrmcrest is located at the heart of the continent of Drakma and serves as the continent's primary center of trade, transportation, and diplomacy. Its central position, fertile lands, and extensive trade networks have made it one of the most prosperous and influential nations in the known world.

Founded over two thousand years ago by King Edric I following his victory over the ancient tyrant Nekros, Wyrmcrest has grown from a heroic kingdom into the economic and political heart of Drakma.

Geography

Terrain

Wyrmcrest occupies the Heartlands, a region characterized by fertile plains, river valleys, forests, and rolling hills. Mountain ranges and rugged cliffs surround much of the kingdom's borders, providing natural defensive barriers against potential invaders.

Climate

The kingdom experiences a variety of climates due to its size and geography. Northern regions, particularly around Winter's Edge and Coreliam Lake, receive regular snowfall during winter, while the southern territories enjoy milder weather thanks to their proximity to the coast.

Natural Resources

Wyrmcrest possesses abundant natural resources, including fertile farmland and rich mineral deposits found along the eastern mountain ranges. These resources form the foundation of the kingdom's wealth and economic stability.

History

The kingdom traces its origins to the end of the Age of Heroes. According to historical records, King Arlan I united the region after defeating the ancient tyrant Nekros, establishing the Kingdom of Wyrmcrest and ushering in a new era of peace and prosperity.

Over the following centuries, Wyrmcrest expanded its influence through diplomacy, trade, and economic development rather than large-scale military conquest.

Population and Culture

As the primary crossroads of Drakma, Wyrmcrest is home to people from nearly every corner of the continent. Merchants, adventurers, craftsmen, scholars, and travelers contribute to a diverse and vibrant society.

Despite its multicultural population, the people of Wyrmcrest share a strong sense of national identity rooted in centuries of common history, trade, and cooperation.

Economy

Trade is the foundation of Wyrmcrest's prosperity. Positioned at the center of major overland and maritime trade routes, the kingdom serves as a gateway connecting the eastern and western regions of Drakma.

Agricultural production, mining operations, manufacturing, and commerce all contribute significantly to the kingdom's economy. Doriam Harbor serves as one of the most important commercial centers in the nation.

Government

Wyrmcrest is governed by a hereditary monarchy that has ruled since the kingdom's founding. While the crown remains the highest authority, local nobles, guild leaders, and influential merchants also play important roles in shaping national policy. The most important ones form the Council that helps the crown rule over different regions.

The Council is composed of the leaders of the four major noble familes of Wyrmcrest, the Hunters Guild Master, the leader of the church of Wyrmcrest, the Comander of the Royal Guard and General of the National Guard.

Foreign Relations

Wyrmcrest maintains generally positive relations with most neighboring nations and frequently acts as a mediator in continental disputes. Its economic influence and extensive trade networks have helped preserve regional stability for centuries.

In recent years, however, diplomatic tensions have increased with the island nation of Pelagia due to allegations of corruption involving government officials and commercial interests.

Notable Locations

Cities and Settlements

  • City of Wyrmcrest — Capital of the kingdom and one of the oldest cities in Drakma.
  • Krestelis City — The second-largest city of the kingdom of Wyrmcrest, known for its coliseum and tournaments.
  • Winter's Edge — Northern frontier city located near the Crown of Fangs mountain range.
  • Doriam Harbor — Major port city and one of the most important commercial centers in the kingdom.
  • Evergreen Village — A small village located close to the southern border between Wyrmcrest and Falkore.
  • Rockbell Village — Mining settlement located at the foot of the eastern mountains.

Natural Regions

  • The Crown of Fangs — A massive mountain chain that cuts the continent, separating the great north from the rest.
  • Crimson Highlands — A region known for its strange red flora, a result of the Vampiric War and its bloodshed.
  • Sunset Mountains — Mountain range forming part of the western frontier.
  • Emerald Mountains — Eastern mountain range known for its rich mineral deposits.
  • Crown Lake — A large lake located on the border of Wyrmcrest and Varlon, Dorian Harbor on its shores.
  • The Howling Canyon — A colossal fissure that cuts through the Crown of Fangs, being the only large and safe land passage through them.
  • The Chasm — A mysterious giant cavity that leads into the underground caverns of Wyrmcrest.
  • Coreliam Lake — Large northern lake famous for its ruined castle and local legends.
  • The Pale Mountains — A set mountains located on the Crown of Fangs, known for their pale coloration and the large birch forest bellow them.
