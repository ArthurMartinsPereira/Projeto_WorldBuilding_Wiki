Overview

Falkore is a large nation located on the eastern side of Drakma, its known especially for its vast forests and rich ecosystems, many unique species of animals, plants and monsters can be found here. Falkore is considered the richest nation when it comes to flora and fauna, they are the largest producers of animal products, spices and fruits in the continent.

Falkore was founded long ago, around the middle of the Age of Heroes. Before that period the people of Falkore were divided among dozens of small tribes, to help her people prosper and survive the goddess Falkia bestowed a blessing to a young shaman, granting her a direct connection to the goddess and a part her power. She eventually unified the tribes and became the ruler of the nation, her descents continue to rule and guide the nation.

Geography

Terrain

The territories that compose Falkore are vastly covered by forests, jungles, rivers and lakes with a few mountains and plateaus in certain areas. At the center of the region is the Heart of Waters, an enormous lake that is sustained by the large river system of the nation. To the north there is the Tempest Mountains and the Crown of Fangs, that separates Falkore from the north, more specifically, from the Wildlands.

Climate

The climate of Falkore is mostly warm and humid, even during the winter temperatures remain stable in most of the nation, though rains become a bit less frequent. Areas like the Tempest Mountains and the Nebulous Jungle receive rain far more frequently than the rest of the nation, this is due to their proximity to the warm eastern sea and the Crown of Fangs mountains.

Natural Resources

As expected of a nation so rich in fauna and flora, most of their resources come from these sources. Magical and medicinal herbs and mushrooms, spices , animal skin meat and bones, certain types of wood and more are all highly valuable for the people of Falkore. Of course, they do value precious metals and gems too, they just priorize things that are more useful in general.

History

Falkore traces its origins to even before the Age of Heroes, to the lost age of humanity, a period where the gods still roamed the land with physical forms. Of course, at the time the people of Falkore were no more than scattered tribes fighting to survive, legends say that after a great cataclysm the forests watered and burned and the air filed with ash and rot.

To save the tribes, Falkia chose to sacrifice her physical form, settling at the heart of the region and turning into the great tree that would become known as the Goddess Tree. Her roots spread across the land, healing the forests and bringing a new breath of life to the land. To this day the tree still stands at the heart of Falkore, the capital of the nation built near its foot.

Population and Culture

Most of the population in Falkore have fairly similar cultures and traditions, though there are some variation from region to region. Aside from regular humans there are also those referred to as the Wildfolk, people blessed by Falkia during birth, they have a greater connection to nature and the forest, being able to commune with or be guided by the goddess. Some Wildfolk can be born with certain anima-like characteristics while still looking mostly human, while other are almost completely human but have special marks across their bodies.

Aside from the native population descended from the tribes that originally inhabited the region, centuries ago many people from the eastern continents came to Falkore, fleeing from the war and destruction blighting their homeland. They were allowed to settle in the Jade Isles, and over time integrated into the nation wile still maintaining the most original culture of their old homeland.

Economy

For the most part Falkore is self sustainable, they produce more than enough food to take care on their people, and technologically they are not too inferior to the neighboring nations. Most of their trading is done with Wyrmcrest, Pelagia and the nations of the Eastern Continents.

They used to do business with Kelrian, but after many conflicts and hostilities they heavily restricted trading with each other. In recent years they have begun to mend their relations, being more friendly to each-other, though tensions are still somewhat high.

Government

Falkore is ruled by a monarchy, more specifically a matriarchal one, the blessing that the royal family carries allows Falkia to occasionally bestow visons or guidance ot their members. It so happens that the female members of this bloodline usually receive clearer visions than the males, so they are prioritized when inheriting the throne, though if there ar no capable female heirs a male can be chosen.

Aside from the monarch other important figures like, shamans and clan heads also have some authority and help rule the nation. The male members of the royal line usually become diplomats or military commanders.

Foreign Relations

Falkore has close ties to a few nations, like Wyrmcrest, Northwind and Heavenrock, while they used to have a neutral relation to Kelrian a few centuries ago hostilities began to grow and escalated into a few conflicts.

From the nations of Drakma, Falkore is the only one that maintains relations with nations of a different continent. More specifically the nations of the Eastern Continents.

Natural Regions

  • The Crown of Fangs — A massive mountain chain that cuts the continent, separating the great north from the rest.
  • Tempest Mountains — Located on the northern side of Falkore, the Tempest Mountains are known for the frequent rains and tropical storms that occur there.

  • Fungal Forest — A small region near the Nebulous Jungle and Tempest Mountains, thanks to the humid climate various species of fungi are able to prosper here.
  • Wild Grove — A large, densely covered, overgrown region, its one of the most hazardous areas of Falkore and is avoidéd by most people.
  • Burnt Glade — The remnants of what used to be an exuberant forest, due to a wildfire that happened roughly a century ago most of the trees were burned away.
