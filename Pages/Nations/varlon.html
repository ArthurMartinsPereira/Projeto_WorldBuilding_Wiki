Overview

Varlon, the nation of vampires, and the smallest country in Drakma. It is the second-youngest nation of the continent, being founded around 750 years ago and receiving sovereignty 615 years ago. Initialy the lands where Varlon exists today used to belong to both Wyrmcrest and Heavenrock, but during the Vampiric War they were conquered for many decades, after the vampires defeat the region remained as a vassal state of the two kingdoms. But after over a hundred years they were given independence.

Varlon was initially founded by the vampire queen Marissa, she wished to create a nation where her children could rule and live without worry. For decades, she prepared an army of vampires by transforming humans, and eventually she struck the fortresses and cities of the region, taking them over before the kingdoms could react. But once Heavenrock and Wyrmcrest united they were able to stop the conquest, the war remained in a stalemate for decades, but eventually Marissa was betrayed and killed. After her death the vampires who surrendered or sided with the two kingdoms were spared.

Geography

Terrain

The lands of Valon mostly include a few mountains, rivers, plains and forests. A part of their territories is covered by part of the Somber Forest, near the border with the Abyss lays the Mount Lunaris, the capital, Lunaxis is located at the heart of the nation. The western shores of Crown Lake is also located within the nations borders.

Climate

The climate in Varlon usually remains stable for most of the year, during winters the north tends to become colder, but it does not snow. Due to 40% of the population being vampires, most large settlements have magic barriers that generate artificial clouds to block or weaken the sun within them, the lands around remain unaffected.

Natural Resources

Varlon's natural resources are not too different from the ones found in the neighboring nations, the only exceptions to this are Dark Stone, the unique flora and fauna of the region, and a mysterious energy found in the underground of the nation.

History

The kingdom traces its origins to the end of the Age of Heroes. According to historical records, King Arlan I united the region after many wars and aliances, establishing the Kingdom of Heavenrock, over the comming centuries more territories were slowly added, including the Abyss, though eventualy it became independent again.

Over the following centuries since its founding, Heavenrock's influence spread across the continent, mostly thanks to the Magic Academy's efforts'. Though after the creation and expancion of the Hunters Guild this influence has steadly diminished.

Population and Culture

The population of Heavenrock is quite simillar to the natives of Wyrmcrest and Varlon, sharing the same cultural roots with them. Heavenrock has always valued the arts and nobility far more than its neighbors, for this reason they neglected anything that seemed brutsh or barbaric to them, there are few proper warriors in Heavenrock asside from the military.

The independence of the Abyss region came due to a large rift forming between them and the rest of the nation, mostly due to them choosing to maintain certain traditions and the growing differences between the culture of the north and south of the nation.

Economy

Most of the economy of Heavenrock is relaiant the magic items they produce and their crops, for most of their history this was enough to sustain the whole nation, though in recent years due to the depletion of many sources of magical materials and ingredients they have been forced to import them, increasing the costs of production drasticaly.

Their agricultural production on the other hand has been triving thanks to their use of golens for heavy labor, they trade their goods mostly with Wyrmcrest, Northwind, Varlon and Pelagia.

Government

Heavenrock is governed by a hereditary monarchy that has ruled since the kingdom's founding. The monarch is the defacto ruller of the nation, with other nobles trying to gain favor with them most of the time.

Over the centuries the Magic Academy gained a lot of power and influence within the nation, being the only other faction that can even consider challeging the crown.

Foreign Relations

Heavenrock tries to maintain positive relations with the neighboring nations, especialy Wyrmcrest, Varlon and Northwind. They also have remained somewhat friendly with the region of the Abyss after its separation from them, on the other hand Dragonfell is still considered their enemy, being the only nation Heavenrock tends to be actively hostile to.

Due to Dragonfell atempting to claim the Abyss for thenselves multiple times, Heavenrock has kept some of its military permanently position near the borders, despite the leaders of the Abyss claiming the can deffend themselves, Heavenrock has insisted in doing so as an "act of generosity" to them.
