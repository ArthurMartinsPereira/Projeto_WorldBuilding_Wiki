Overview

Varlon is the homeland of the vampires and the smallest sovereign nation on the continent of Drakma. Founded roughly 750 years ago and recognized as an independent kingdom 615 years ago, it is also the continent's second-youngest nation.

The lands that now form Varlon once belonged to both Wyrmcrest and Heavenrock. During the Vampiric War they were conquered by Queen Marissa and her followers, who sought to establish a homeland where vampires could live free from persecution. Although Marissa's conquest ultimately failed, the territory remained a joint vassal of its former rivals for more than a century before eventually gaining independence. Modern Varlon is known for the peaceful coexistence between humans and vampires, its advanced medical knowledge, and its policy of neutrality in continental affairs.

Geography

Terrain

Varlon is characterized by fertile plains, rolling woodlands, gentle rivers, and scattered mountain ranges. The nation encompasses part of the Somber Forest, while the western shores of Crown Lake lie within its borders. Along its eastern frontier, overlooking the Abyss, rises Mount Lunaris, one of the country's most recognizable landmarks. At the heart of the nation stands Lunaxis, its capital and largest city.

Climate

Varlon's climate is generally mild throughout the year. Winters are cooler in the northern regions, though snowfall is extremely rare. Because nearly forty percent of the population consists of vampires, most major settlements are protected by magical barriers that generate artificial cloud cover during daylight hours. These enchantments shield vampires from direct sunlight while leaving the surrounding countryside largely unaffected. As a result, Varlon's cities often appear perpetually overcast, a distinctive sight that has become synonymous with the nation itself.

Natural Resources

Due to its relatively small territory, Varlon lacks many natural resources in significant quantities. To offset this limitation, the nation relies on several rare resources found almost exclusively within its borders, including Dark Stone, Lunar Silver, the mysterious Blood Gems, and a wide variety of native plants and wildlife that support its renowned medical and alchemical industries.

History

For most of recorded history, the lands that now form Varlon were little more than remote border provinces shared between Wyrmcrest and Heavenrock. Considered strategically unimportant by both kingdoms, these territories received little attention or investment. This prolonged neglect allowed Queen Marissa and her followers to establish hidden strongholds, quietly building their strength until they launched their conquest during the Vampiric War.

Early during the war, the vampires were aggressively conquering towns, cities and other settlements in the region, and as the war prolonged these were altered to suit the new inhabitants. After the war was over tensions between humans and the vampires that were left were still high, what lead to a great division among both populations for generations. Though after they gained their independence both groups became more united.

Population and Culture

Varlon's population is constituted of mostly regular humans and vampires, now days both sides are much closer than they used to though there are still many cultural diferences that developed between them over time.

Vampires mostly live in the larger settlements of the nation due to them being designed to support them, while most of the human population inhabits the countryside, in recent years though, many more humans have been moving into the cities.

Economy

Varlon's economy relies on agriculture, mining, medicine, and international trade. The nation's physicians are regarded as the finest in Drakma, largely because vampire Blood Magic has significantly advanced surgical techniques, regenerative treatments, and the study of anatomy.

The country exports medicinal supplies, Lunar Silver, and refined Dark Stone while importing manufactured goods and luxury products. Twilight Wine from Wyrmcrest has become particularly popular among both humans and vampires.

Government

During its founding Varlon was originaly ruled by a sole monarch, Queen Marissa, the progenitor of vampire kind. She had absolute control over the nation, the only other people with power were the heads of each vampire house and the crowned prince.

After Marissa's betrayal and defeat, Varlon remined as a vassal state of Wyrmcrest and Heavenrock for over a century, after being granted independence a council was formed. To prevent either race from dominating the other, the Council consists of five representatives from the remaining vampire houses and five elected human leaders. Major legislation requires support from both groups.

Foreign Relations

Varlon has maintained neutral relations with most nations since the Vampiric War, preferring to avoid conflicts. This has worked well for them, especially when it comes to commerce and diplomacy.

Varlon's closest alies are Wyrmcrest and Heavenrock, Wyrmcrest in particular trades a lot of goods with Varlon, the three nations have also supported each-other in times of need. Though Heavenrock is still a bit resentful for the lands and resources they've lost, they have bigger concerns than trying to fight Varlon.
