Nations of the World

A catalog of all the known nations in each continent.

Drakma

Wyrmcrest

A prosperous Heartlands kingdom and the center of trade in Drakma. Wyrmcrest controls many major trade routes and is home to the Hunters Guild.

Dragonfell

A young desert nation forged from the unification of former tribes. Though its raiding days are over, tensions with Heavenrock remain.

Falkore

A vast eastern nation of forests and jungles, known for its remarkable biodiversity and strong ties with neighboring allies.

Northwind

The largest nation in Drakma, stretching beyond the Crown of Fangs. Its people are renowned for their resilience and warrior traditions.

Varlon

The smallest nation in Drakma and home to a large vampire population. Founded after the Vampiric War, it later achieved full sovereignty.

Heavenrock

Known as the Nation of Magic, Heavenrock is the birthplace of Path Magic and home to some of the continent's most prestigious magical institutions.

Garmorys

A mineral-rich nation famous for its master smiths and artisans. The vast cave systems beneath its lands are known as Underworld.

Pelagia

An island nation known for innovation and engineering. Airships and elemental automatons were first developed here.

Kelrian

A secluded nation of marshes and swamps. Kelrian pioneered the development of firearms and magical weaponry.

Independent or Semi-Independent Regions

The Abyss

A culturally distinct region between Heavenrock and Dragonfell. Though once part of Heavenrock, it now operates largely independently.

The Wild Frontier

Also known as the Wildlands, its the remote eastern frontier of Northwind. Its harsh climate has left the region sparsely populated by nomadic tribes.

Ancestral Falls

A region of islands and colossal oceanic waterfalls surrounding Titan's Gravestone. It serves as one of the safest northern sea routes in Drakma.
