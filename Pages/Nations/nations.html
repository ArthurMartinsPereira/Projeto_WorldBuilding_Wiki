Nations of the World

A catalog of all the known nations in each continent.

Drakma

Wyrmcrest

A prosperous kingdom located on the heart of the continent(also known as the Heartlands). Wyrmcrest is known as the center of commerce and trade across Drakma, controlling most of the important trading routes between the east and west of the continent, it's also known as the home of the Hunters Guild.

Dragonfell

A relatively young nation formed by the many tribes that inhabit the deserts of Dragonfell. Its people used to be raiders in the past but have since tried to leave behind that past, though some neighboring nations still are careful with them, especialy Heavenrock who is still quite hostile with Dragonfell.

Falkore

A large nation located to the east of the Heartlands, most of Falkore's land is covered by dense forests and jungles, it's the most biodiverse place in the continent. Falkore is relatively neutral with other nations, its only alies are Wyrmcrest and Northwind, in the recent past they have gone through a war against Kelrian due to territorial disputes.

Northwind

The largest nation of Drakma, its territories include all the nothern lands lacated beyond the Crown of Fangs mountain chain. The people of Northwind are both harsh but welcoming towards outsiders. Due to their isolation compared to the other nations they have almost never participated in any large scale conflicts since the unification of the north. But Northwind remains known as the home of some of the gratest warriors in the continent.

Varlon

A small nation nestled between Heavenrock and Wyrmcrest, Varlon is the smallest nation of Drakma, it was founded centuries ago after the Vampiric war. After the defeat of the vampires Varlon remained as a vassal state of both Wyrmcrest and Heavenrock, but eventualy they were granted soverenty. Around 40% to 45% of its population are vampires, though most aren't pure blood ones.

Heavenrock

Located to the west of the Heartland lies the kingdom of Heavenrock, also known as the Nation of Magic, it's the birthplace of Path Magic and Home of the Magic Academy of Heavenrock. When it comes to magic, Heavenrock is the nation that is most reliant of it in Drakma, from farm work to building, they use magic on everything there. Though still quite powerful and influent it has been slowly declining over the past few decades, on the other hand Wyrmcrest has remained at the top.

Garmorys

Located just bellow the deserts of Dragonfell lies Garmorys, a nation known for having the best artisans and smiths in the whole continent. Garmorys is extremely rich when it comes to mineral resorces, it's lands have many mountains, vulcanos, platos, ravines and caves, they have so many caves bellow their nation that they connect into what is known as the Underworld, Garmorys is technicaly the oldest nation in the continent, with its recorded history dating back to the very start of the Age of Heroes, though it has changed much over millenia. The people of Garmorys are also known for being very tall and strong, the tallest humans in the continent.

Pelagia

An arquipelago nation composed of 7 islands, although small Pelagia has been fighting for a space among the other big nations of Drakma, recently they have invented many machines and technologies that have been helping the suplement their weak points, the two most notable ones being airships and the elemental automatons. Despite being in a period of prosperity, Pelagia suffers from great inequality between classes and corruption among nobility and government officials, this has lead to the organization of militias and rebel groups.

Kelrian

Located south of Falkore, Kelrian is a nation mostly composed of swamps and marshlands, they are not particularly known for being active in global afairs, preffering to keep to themselves. Kelrian was the first nation to develop firearms, and eventualy even integrate magic in them. The only major conlficts Kelrian has actively taken part in was their war against Falkore around a century ago.
