Overview

Dragonfell is the youngest nation in Drakma, founded roughly 300 years ago in the vast western deserts south of the Abyss. Before its unification, the region was inhabited by dozens of nomadic tribes that survived through trade, warfare, and raids against neighboring kingdoms.

Today, Dragonfell remains a tribal monarchy ruled by a king or queen who must earn the throne through the Three Trials rather than inherit it. Though united under a single banner, the tribes retain much of their independence, traditions, and identities.

Geography

Terrain

The lands of Dragonfell are mostly composed of large deserts and dunes of sand, though there are a few notable areas, like the Salt Plains, the Badlands, the Pearlescent Coast and a few oasis scattered around. At the heart of the desert lays the city of Dragonfell, the city was built in an area shielded by large cliffs and mountains, and at the center there are the fossilized remains of what seems to have been a colossal dragon.

Climate

In the vast majority of Dragonfell the climate is dry and hot, there is little rain throughout the year, with the only locations that receive them more often being the eastern and western coastal regions and the borders with the Abyss and Varlon. Sand storms are relatively frequent across the nation, with them being almost constant in the region of the Spiral Valley.

Natural Resources

Due to being located in a desertic region, Dragonfell lacks most of the natural resources that are considered common in other nations, they mostly commercialize spices, salt a few ores and certain native herbs and grains. In recent years they have begun to commercialize Cliffbeans, that can be used to make an energizing beverage.(similar to coffee)

History

Most of Dragonfell's history is from the period they were not a unified nation, from the time when tribes still were distrustful towards each-other, this history is usually kept by the story tellers and keepers of each tribe, and retold in the forms of stories and legends.

In these there are a variety of tales, from legendary heroes to monsters and vengeful spirits. Some legends even include fragments of information of ancient civilizations that used to inhabit the deserts. After their unification most of the history that is recorded is related to their leaders, conflicts and other important events.

Population and Culture

Although the most of the tribes have united and started working together after the 300 years since the unification,the population and culture of Dragonfell is still very divided, most tribes still choose to keep a degree of independence, maintaining their traditions, and culture while still working with the others.

The 8 Great Tribes are the largest and most influential ones, most of the smaller tribes work under them or are branches of them. There also have been a few tribes that choose to not unite with the rest, to this day they still live isolated and hiden in the deserts, keeping to the old ways, and occasionally raiding caravans and travelers.

Economy

The economy of Dragonfell is still limited, due to their past as raiders some nations still have reservations when making business with them. Though since Wyrmcrest has been more receptive to their efforts, other nations have started to open up too. Dragonfell mostly exports spices, salt, grains and some rare desert minerals.

Heavenrock is the only neighboring nation that remains hostile towards Dragonfell, doing very little commerce with them. Most of the Heavenrock goods that end up in Dragonfell were either traded with others or smuggled.

Government

Dragonfell is ruled by a monarch with the help of a council composed of the leaders of each one of the 8 Great Tribes that united during the founding of the nation. The throne is not hereditary though, once a ruler dies or is expelled the next is chosen by going through the Three Trials.

The Three Trials were originaly created by the trio of gods that rule over the region of Dragonfell, each trial is designed to test something, Strength, Leadership, Wisdom. Anyone from Dragonfell can take the trials as long as they are allowed to by the gods. One can not even enter the Spire of Trials without permition.

Foreign Relations

In the past, the neighboring nations of Dragonfell were mostly defencive or hostile when dealing with the tribes. But since the unification and founding of Dragonfell as a propper nation, most nations have become more friendly, though they still may have some reservations when dealing with Dragonfell.

The only nation that has remained outright hostile to Dragonfell is Heavenrock, they have not forgotten the past, and due to Dragonfell's attempts at claiming the region of the Abyss they have come into conflict many times.

Notable Locations

Cities and Settlements

  • Dragonfell City — The Capital city of Dragonfell and home to the Pale Palace, located at heart of the desert it was built around the fossilized reminats of what apears to have been a colossal dragon.
  • Heron Harbor — The main trading hub of the nation, located on the Pearlescent Coast, it's also known for being the breadbasket of the nation, having fertile lands around it.
  • Pharis Port — A small costal city build on the Sea Serpent Bay, it mostly trades with Wyrmcrest, Varlon and Pelagia.
  • Dunefall — A city build on the edge of the Abyss, mostly known for the massive sandfalls that flow into the Abyss only to be blown of by the winds.
  • Dustbound — A large city located in the region of the Badlands, used to be completely lawless, but was eventually incorporated into Dragonfell.
  • Helixhold — The largest settlement in the region of the Spiral Valley, it's the main trading hub for goods coming from or going to Garmorys.

Natural Regions

  • Spiral Valley — An region located on the border with the nation of Garmorys, known for its strange geography and the endless sand storm at its heart on the Storm Pinnacle.
  • Sunscorched Cliffs — A arid stretch of land covered by large red sandstone cliffs and small rocky mountains. Cliffbeans are natural to this area
  • Salt Plains — A large salt desert, although mostly empty there are a few notable locations like the Salt Lakes and the Roseate Basin.
  • Pearlescent Coast — The western coast of the nation, especially known for producing high quality pearls, shells and other sea related goods.
  • Sea Serpent Bay — The eastern coast of the nation, much smaller than the western, but still very important for trading.
  • The Badlands — A large red desert located near the Abyss, slightly more habitable than the Sea of Dunes, but a large part of it was destroyed and became known as the Molten Badlands.
  • Termal Oasis — An large oasis region, filled with healing termal springs and lakes, a popular tourist destination.
  • The Sea of Dunes — Comprising most of Dragonfell's territories, this region is mostly made up of dunes and sand, with only a few valleys and mountainous regions. Many things seem to have been buried by these sands.
  • The Sunken Veil — A recently discovered massive underground aquifer right bellow the Salt Plains, filled with its own fauna and flora and what appears to be the ruins of an ancient civilization.

Landmarks and Points of Interest

  • The Pale Palace — Located at the center of Dragonfell city is the Pale Palace, a fortress built in and around the skull of a giant dragon fossil, the seat of power of the nation's ruler and the location of the Ebony Throne.
  • Storm Pinnacle — A strangely shaped mountain at the heart of the Spiral Valley, seemingly the source of perpetual sand storm that cover the region. Also, the nest of the Spiral Terror.
  • Salt Lakes — A group of shallow lakes that form in the Salt Plains after the rainy season.
  • The Roseate Basin — A part of the Salt Plains that for some reason has turned into varying tones of reds and pinks.
  • The Spire of Trials — A enormous tower that was created by the three gods of Dragonfell, here is where any potential king or queen goes to take the Three Trials.
