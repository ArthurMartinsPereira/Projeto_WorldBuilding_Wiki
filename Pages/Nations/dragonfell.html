The youngest nation of Drakma, located in the large eastern desert.

Overview

Dragonfell is the youngest nation in Drakma, founded roughly 300 years ago in the vast eastern deserts south of the Abyss. Before its unification, the region was inhabited by dozens of nomadic tribes that survived through trade, warfare, and raids against neighboring kingdoms.

Today, Dragonfell remains a tribal monarchy ruled by a king or queen who must earn the throne through the Three Trials rather than inherit it. Though united under a single banner, the tribes retain much of their independence, traditions, and identities.

Geography

Terrain

The lands of Dragonfell are mostly composed of large deserts and dunes of sand, though there are a few notable areas, like the Salt Plains, the Badlands, the eastern coast and a few oasis scatered around. At the heart of the desert lays the city of Dragonfell, the city was built in an area shilded by large cliffs and mountains, and at the center there are the fossilized remains of what seems to have been a colosal dragon.

Climate

In the vast majority of Dragonfell the climate is dry and hot, there is little rain though out the year, with the only locations that recieve them more eftenly being the eastern and western coastal regions. Sand storms are relatively common, with them being almost constant in the region of the Spiral Valley

Natural Resources

Dragonfell lacks most natural resources that are considered common in other nations, they mostly comertialize spices, salt a few ores and certain native herbs and crops. (In recent years they have began to comertialize the beans of a plant with simillar characteristics to coffe)

History

Most of Dragonfell's history are from the period they were not a unified nation, this history is usualy kept by the tribes, and retold in the forms of stories and legends.

In these there are a variety of tales, from legendary heroes to monsters and vengeful spirits. Some legends even include fragments of informations of ancient civilizations that used to inhabit the deserts.

Population and Culture

The population and culture of Dragonfell is still very divided, although the tribes have been united for 300 hundred years, most choose to keep a degree of independence, keeping their traditions, and culture while still working with others.

There also have been a few tribes that choose to not unite with the rest, to this day they still live isolated in the deserts, keeping to the old ways, and ocasionaly rading caravans and travelers.

Economy

The economy of Dragonfell is still limited, due to their past as raiders some nations still have reservations when making business with them. Though since Wyrmcrest has been more receptive to their efforts other nations have started to open up too.

Heavenrock is the only neighboring nation that remains a bit hostile towards Dragonfell, doing very little commerce with them. Most of the Heavenrock goods that end up in the nations were either traded with others or smuggled.

Government

Dragonfell is ruled by a monarch with the help of a council composed of the leaders of each one of the 8 Great Tribes that united during the founding of the nation.

The throne is not hereditary though, once a ruler dies or is expeled the next is chosen by going through the Three Trials created by triade of gods of Dragonfell, each is designed to test something, Strenght, Leadership, Erudition. Anyone from Dragonfell can take the test as long as they are accepted by the deities.
