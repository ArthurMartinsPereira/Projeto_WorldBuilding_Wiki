Overview

Dragonfell is the youngest nation in Drakma, founded only three centuries ago in the vast western deserts south of the Abyss. For much of their history, the people of the region lived as independent nomadic tribes that frequently raided neighboring kingdoms such as Heavenrock and Wyrmcrest.

This changed when a legendary chieftain united the tribes under a single banner and established the Kingdom of Dragonfell. Though united, the tribes retain much of their independence and cultural identity. The kingdom is ruled by a monarch chosen through the sacred Three Trials rather than hereditary succession, a tradition believed to be guided by Dragonfell's patron gods.

Geography

Terrain

The lands of Dragonfell are mostly composed of large deserts and dunes of sand, though there are a few notable areas, like the Salt Plains, the Badlands, the eastern coast and a few oasis scatered around. At the heart of the desert lays the city of Dragonfell, the city was built in an area shilded by large cliffs and mountains, and at the center there are the fossilized remains of what seems to have been a colosal dragon.

Climate

Dragonfell has an arid desert climate characterized by extreme heat during the day and cold nights. Rainfall is rare and often arrives in short but intense seasonal storms. Most settlements rely on oasis, underground aquifers, or seasonal rivers for water.

Along the eastern coast, temperatures are milder and humidity is higher, allowing for more stable agriculture and larger permanent settlements.

Natural Resources

Despite its harsh environment, Dragonfell produces several valuable goods. These include desert spices, medicinal herbs, salt, rare ores, gemstones, and drought-resistant crops.

In recent decades, the cultivation of cliffbeans has become one of the kingdom's fastest-growing industries. The roasted seeds produce a stimulating beverage popular throughout Drakma.

History

Much of Dragonfell's history predates the kingdom itself. Before unification, knowledge was preserved by tribal storytellers and keepers who passed down events through oral traditions.

These stories contain accounts of legendary heroes, ancient battles, monsters, spirits, and forgotten civilizations that once inhabited the desert. While many tales are considered myths, some have proven to contain fragments of genuine historical events.

Population and Culture

Despite three centuries of unity, tribal identity remains a central part of life in Dragonfell. Most tribes govern their own affairs, maintain unique customs, and preserve traditions that predate the kingdom itself.

Not all tribes joined the kingdom. Several continue to roam the deep desert independently, rejecting the authority of the crown. These isolated tribes follow older customs and are known to occasionally raid caravans and travelers crossing their territories.

Economy

The economy of Dragonfell is still limited, due to their past as raiders some nations still have reservations when making business with them. Though since Wyrmcrest has been more receptive to their efforts other nations have started to open up too.

Heavenrock is the only neighboring nation that remains a bit hostile towards Dragonfell, doing very little commerce with them. Most of the Heavenrock goods that end up in the nations were either traded with others or smuggled.

Government

Dragonfell is ruled by a monarch with the help of a council composed of the leaders of each one of the 8 Great Tribes that united during the founding of the nation.

The throne is not hereditary though, once a ruler dies or is expeled the next is chosen by going through the Three Trials created by triade of gods of Dragonfell, each is designed to test something, Strenght, Leadership, Wisdom. Anyone from Dragonfell can take the test as long as they are accepted by the deities.

Foreign Relations

Now days Dragonfell has friendly relations with Wyrmcrest, Pelagia and Garmorys. The other nations are all neutral in relation to them, the only one that remains somewhat hostile is Heavenrock, who has not forgoten their past.

Since the founding of Dragonfell the only wars they fought were against Heavenrock, they were atempting to claim the region of the Abyss, that had recently become independent of Heavenrock. In response to that Heavenrock sent their armies to fight Dragonfell, since then they have had ocasional fights.
