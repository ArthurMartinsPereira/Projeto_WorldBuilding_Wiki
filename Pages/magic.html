Magic in Drakma

A catalog containing the fundamentals of magic and how it affects the world.

Elemental Energy

The energy that composes magic, can be divided in 10 different elements. Ignis, Animus, Electro, Zephyr, Geo, Aqua and Cryo compose the 7 Lesser/Natural Elements, while Light, Darkness and Arcane compose the 3 Higher Elements.

Primal Magic

Primal magic describes the most basic and instinctive kind of control over magic, the one creatures, plants, monsters and humans, capable of using magic, have access to. Primal Magic is guided by instinct, imagination and emotions.

Path Magic

A more structured magic system when compared to Primal Magic, it was created to serve as a more reliable and stable way of using magic, spells, enchantments and magic tools are all creations of Path Magic.

Outer Magic

This form of magic is conected to powers beyond the natural realm, this kind of magic can bend or violate the rules of the universe and is far more powerful and rare than Primal and Path Magic. Usualy Outer Magic is conected to the Gods and Descenders.

The Lay Lines

A web of magic veins that runs across the entire world, it not only contains magic and keeps it in a stable form, but also distributes it across different regions, shapping the terrain, climate and ecology. They are essential for the balance of the world, the ones located in Drakma are particularly powerful.
