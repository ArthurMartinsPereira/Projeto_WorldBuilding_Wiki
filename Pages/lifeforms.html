Lifeforms of Drakma

Catalog of known living beings across the continent.

Animals

Natural fauna inhabiting forests, oceans, mountains, deserts, etc. Also includes domesticated animals.

Monsters

Dangerous creatures known for aggression, mutations and supernatural origins and unpredictable behavior.

Elementals

Beings formed from the elemental enegy of the Lay Lines, Can range from simple barely consious blobs of energy to powerful and more complex forms.

Flora

Plant life ranging from common vegetation to dangerous or magical species.
