The Flora of Drakma

Catalog of the known notable flora found across Drakma

Monstrosity

These are creatures that have somehow been mutated, maybe by magic or by artificial means. They all are known for having monstrous forms, most of the time they are only as smart as beasts, but some rare few can reach higher levels of intellect.

Undead

The undead are an elusive and hard to deal kind of monsters, they include beings ranging from spirits to walking corpses and even amalgamations. Most are extremely aggressive and difficult to take down, while some rare few can be neutral or good.

Constructs

Not to be confused with the Elemental Constructs, this group mostly includes artificial creations like golens and certain kinds of machines. Most of the time they don't possess sentience and only follow orders given by their creators or owners.

Mythical

These kinds of monsters are extremely powerful and rare, many become legendary figures in various cultures. Usually the only ones that are placed in this category are Dragons and Hallowed Aberrations, though in rare cases some familiars and other kinds of beings might reach this level.
