The Flora of Drakma

Catalog of the known notable flora found across Drakma

Blood Blossoms

A kind of flower known for growing in places where a lot of blood was spilled or many deaths happened. Is one of the best known ingredients for healing elixirs or potions.

Ardenias

A dangerous kind of tree found exclusively in the swamps of Kelrian, its sap is filled with chemicals and elemental energy that react aggressively with water, boiling it, its fruits have a tendency to explode to disperse seeds. These fruits can be used in the creation of a special kind of gunpowder.

Petrawood Trees

A group of trees belonging to the Petra Flora family, they possess a hard mineralized bark similar to stone. During winter this bark compresses and closes around the trees, acting as a protective shell against the cold of the Wild Frontier.

Mandrakes

A kind of small herb composed mostly of its roots, its extremely toxic, being able to poison someone from just trying to hold it with bare hands. Despite this toxicity, the core of the plant's roots can be used in various kinds of potions.
