The Flora of Drakma

Catalog of the known notable flora found across Drakma

Monstrosity

These are creatures that have somehow been mutated, maybe by magic or by artificial means. They all are known for having monstrous forms, most of the time they are completely feral, with not even their creators being able to control them.

Undead

The undead are an elusive and hard to deal kind of monsters, they include beings ranging from spirits to walking corpses and even amalgamations. Most are extremely aggressive and difficult to take down, while some rare few can be neutral or good.

Artificial Beings

Not to be confused with the Elemental Constructs, this group mostly includes artificial creations like Golens and certain kinds of machines, or certain artificial lifeforms, like Familiars. Many of these don't possess true sentience and tend to follow orders from their creators, though there are exceptions.

Mythical

These kinds of monsters are extremely powerful and rare, many become legendary figures in various cultures. Usually the only ones that are placed in this category are Dragons and Hallowed Aberrations, though in rare cases some Familiars and other kinds of beings might reach this level.
