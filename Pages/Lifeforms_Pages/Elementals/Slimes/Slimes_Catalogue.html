Slime Catalog

A catalog of the different kinds of slime that exist across Drakma.

Overview

Most slimes share the same traits with only a few specific changes depending on the element they are composed of. They all possess shapeless gelatinous forms composed of pure elemental energy, the consistency of their bodies varies according to the element.
