Ground Slime

One of the many kinds of slime found across Drakma, known for their affinity to the earth element.

Overview

Ground Slimes are a common variant of slime found all across Drakma, like all slimes their bodies are amorphous blobs of elemental energy, they don't possess any sensory organs, or just organs in general. They visualize the world through the lay lines, only reacting to external stimulus, they prefer to roam around areas where the earth element is stronger, like mountains caves, canions, etc.

They are particularly common near mountainous regions, not necessarily on top of the mountains, but in the surrounding area. Like other slimes, if they become old enough they can evolve into a more advanced elemental lifeform, or split into smaller ones.

Appearance

Their bodies can vary in appearance depending on where they are living, in general they look as if made of mud or some other kind of earthy sludge. In deserts and beaches they can look as if made of sand and in caves gravel and rocks.

Their elemental core is located deep within their bodies, it looks like and orb glowing in brown/orange tones. Their cores are particularly hard, being extremely dense and difficult to damage.

The bodies of ground slimes are often covered in rocks, mud chips, or larger pieces of terrain embedded in them. Compared to other slimes their bodies are quite firm, they can also use their energy to temporarily harden the outer layer of their bodies or to create a kind of earth armor to protect themselves.

Nature

Ground slimes are the most sluggish of their kind, they can spend days without moving or doing anything, in some cases they stay still for so long plants start growing over their bodies. In combat though, they can be descriptively fast once enough of their armor has been chipped away.

Throughout their lives ground slimes can accumulate minerals in their bodies, these help they become more resilient over time, but also put a target on them, some people hunt them just to extract these valuable mineral deposits.

Abilities

These are the main abilities of ground slimes:

  • Hardening - They can temporarily harden their outer layer, creating a kind of armor that protects them from the majority of attacks.
  • Rock Spike: Their bodies can have many hidden spikes composed of their magic and the minerals they absorb, these can shoot out when they attack or are attacked.
  • Digging: When resting or fleeing they can dig to hide underground, or to strike from bellow.

Behavior

All slimes are pretty basic lifeforms, since they don't have sensory organs or follow the rules of biology they tend to look alien to some. Most of the time they just roam around, slowly sliding across the ground while occasionally feeding from the lay lines.

They only react to external stimuli, seeing the world through the information recorded within the lay lines. So if someone or something touches or hits them, they will not really feel it, they simply sense something and react according to the type of stimuli.

In the case of ground slimes they are the laziest of their kind and many times may not even feel an attack, due to their armor. But if they notice a threat they can shoot hardened projectiles.

Crystallization

In rare cases, a matured Ground Slime might not go through the process of evolution or splitting. Instead, they simply continue to grow and absorb minerals, eventually the pressure within them grows to the point it begins to crystallize the minerals into special formations. These are highly valuable in the craft on magical items.

Habitat

Ground slimes can be found living in almost any region, though they favor areas with a more rocky geology. They can appear due to lay line instability too. The ones found in the underground tend to be larger and have more mineral deposits, making them much more resilient than normal.

Ecology

Slimes are the base of the ecology for elemental lifeforms, being the weakest and most primitive, but also the starting point for most others elementals.

In the case of Ground Slimes, they are extremely common across the continent and in many places their presence helps to move minerals through the soil and spread them around evenly, allowing the plants and animals to prosper.

Many species of drakes hunt these slimes to consume the minerals they accumulate inside their bodies. though most of the time they avoid killing the slime, merely extracting the minerals and consuming them, what helps their own armored scales and plates develop more healthily.

Relationship with Humans

Humans tend to see Slimes as nothing more than pests, but in the case of Ground Slimes, many tend to tolerate their presence, especially due to how passive they are.

Many farmers keep small controlled populations to help them turn the earth of their plantations and keep the soil healthy. There are even Rock Farmers, who specifically take care of these slimes to use them to extract minerals from the ground. An authorization from the Hunters Guild is required for these activities though.
