Fulgur Slime

One of the many kinds of slime found across Drakma, known for their affinity to the Electro element.

Overview

Fulgur Slimes are one of the more elusive variants of slime, like all slimes their bodies are just blobs of elemental energy, they don't possess any organs and only can see the world through the lay lines. The closest thing to an organ they have is their elemental core.

These slimes are mostly found in areas of high elevation or where storms are frequent, though mainly, any place with charged enough electricity can attract these creatures. This trait has lead them to become attracted to many modern human machinery, often causing electrical or mechanical problems.

Appearance

Fulgur Slimes generally have the signature gelatinous appearances that all slimes have, but on their case their bodies are also constantly crackling with electricity, faintly glowing and emitting sparks.

Their core is located deep within their gelatinous bodies, it can glow in blue, yellow or purple tones, it shines very brightly even when compared to other cores.

As they age Fulgur Slimes become more and more charged, older individuals carry so much electricity that they begin to hover a little above the ground, looking like spheres of lightning.

Nature

These slimes become more and more charged as they age, the gel that composes their bodies is extremely efficient at trapping electrical charge without it dispersing. They do lose some energy over time, but the amount they gain is greater than the loss, being a net positive. This ability to trap electricity is what allows them to be in contact with the ground without losing their charge.

When they reach the end of their lifespan these slimes either split into smaller ones or evolve into a higher category of elemental lifeform. Their evolved forms tend to be particularly dangerous depending on how much charge they had before evolving.

Abilities

Fulgur slimes share abilities with others of their kind but also have a few unique ones.

  • High Voltage - The gelatinous mass that compose their bodies is charged with so much energy that physical attacks only lead to the attacker being electrocuted.
  • Magnetism: As they age and their bodies gain more charge they eventually become somewhat magnetic. Some are even capable of covering themselves in metal as a protective measure.
  • Concentrated Discharge: They can release massive discharges of electricity when threatened or hurt. Targets coated in their slime are especially vulnerable.

Behavior

All slimes are pretty basic lifeforms, since they don't have sensory organs or follow the rules of biology they tend to look alien to some. Most of the time they just roam around, slowly sliding across the ground while occasionally feeding from the lay lines.

They only react to external stimuli, seeing the world through the information recorded within the lay lines. So if someone or something touches or hits them, they will not really feel it, they simply sense something and react according to the type of stimuli.

In the case of Fulgur Slimes though, they are actually also capable of "feeling" the electric field of living beings, granting them a kind of limited "vison". This makes their attacks far more accurate than those of other slimes.

Environmental Effects

Do to their presence, any environment they spend enough time in starts to become more saturated with the electro element. Usually this manifests as things such as fulgurite forming on the fround, or metals becoming magnetized. In the most extreme cases though, magnetic anomalies and constant electrical discharges have been observed to happen.

Habitat

Fulgur Slimes are known for inhabiting areas with high elevation, constant thunderstorms, or at least a great amount of the electro element. Sometimes groups of Fulgur slimes will coverage and pile up creating something akin to a lighting rod, this busts their capacity to attract absorb and channel energy. Occasionally they can also spawn in other locations due to lay line instabilities.

In recent times they also have started to be found living in man-made structures or vehicles. particularly the more modern ones that are starting to use electric systems.

Ecology

Slimes are the base of the ecology for elemental lifeforms, being the weakest and most primitive, but also the starting point for most others elementals.

Fulgur Slimes are relatively uncommon in most places, but over abundant in areas where electricity, natural or artificial is found. Their very presence shapes these places, magnetizing the minerals in the ground and, causing magnetic and electric disturbances and more.

Relationship with Humans

Humans tend to see Slimes as nothing more than pests, in the case of Toxic Slimes, due to their overall rarity, humans on average interact with them frequently. Though adventurers, hunters or explorers may find them in the wild.

In recent times people have begun to extract the gel produced by Fulgur Slimes to use it in special batteries, thanks to its ability to store massive amounts of electricity, these have a great capacity. The problem is controlling the release of said energy, since it tends to be quite volatile.

The Research Institute of Pelagia has been using elemental cores from slimes, including Fulgur Slimes, to power machines and automatons. They are also the ones behind the stu of their special gel.
