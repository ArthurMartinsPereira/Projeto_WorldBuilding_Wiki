Fulgur Slime

One of the many kinds of slime found across Drakma, known for their affinity to the Electro element.

Overview

Fulgur Slimes are one of the more elusive variants of slime, like all slimes their bodies are just blobs of elemental energy, they don't possess any organs and only can see the world through the lay lines. The closest thing to an organ they have is their elemental core.

These slimes are mostly found in areas where nature has gone rampant and grown out of control. Places like jungles, deep forests and abandoned overgrown ruins are their favourite spots to hide.

Appearance

Toxic Slimes generally have a pretty gooey appearance, their consistency being somewhere between the Aqua Slimes and Ground Slimes. They come in various shades of green, varying depending on their environment and growth stage.

Their core is located deep within their gelatinous bodies, it glows in a green coloration and is less gelatinous than the rest of their bodies.

As they age Toxic Slimes not only grow in size but in toxicity too, once they become old enough their bodies become somewhat acidic too.

Nature

These slimes tend to become more and more toxic as they age, eventually they even start to become acidic. Funnily enough this toxicity is only harmful to animals and people, but not plant life, often having the opposite effect on them and stimulating growth.

When they reach the end of their lifespan these slimes either split into smaller ones or evolve into a higher category of elemental lifeform.

Abilities

Toxic slimes share abilities with others of their kind but also have a few unique ones.

  • Toxic Mass - The gelatinous mass that compose their bodies is extremely toxic to animals and people, just contact with skin can be dangerous.
  • Acidity: As they age their bodies become somewhat acidic in nature, being able to slowly dissolve organic matter if in contact with it for too long.
  • Flora Overgrowth: The slime they leave behind as they move carries a large quantity of animus energy, stimulating and accelerating the growth of plant life.

Behavior

All slimes are pretty basic lifeforms, since they don't have sensory organs or follow the rules of biology they tend to look alien to some. Most of the time they just roam around, slowly sliding across the ground while occasionally feeding from the lay lines.

They only react to external stimuli, seeing the world through the information recorded within the lay lines. So if someone or something touches or hits them, they will not really feel it, they simply sense something and react according to the type of stimuli.

In the case of Toxic Slimes, when aggravated they will attempt to launch themselves at the aggressor, sticking to them in an attempt to poison or dissolve them. They are also capable of shooting poisonous projectiles, though their aim isn't the best.

Environmental Adaptations

Depending on the location they are inhabiting, their toxin may take different characteristics, raging from neurotoxin to even necrosis in the worst cases. The consistency, composition and other traits of their bodies may also change depending on the environment.

Habitat

Toxic Slimes prefer inhabiting locations rich in the animus element, such as forests, jungles or overgrown ruins. The more nature around the more suitable the location. Their presence also tends to accelerate plant growth to an abnormal degree, leading to areas becoming completely overgrown. Occasionally they can also spawn in other locations due to lay line instabilities.

Ecology

Slimes are the base of the ecology for elemental lifeforms, being the weakest and most primitive, but also the starting point for most others elementals.

Toxic Slimes are relatively rare when compared to others of their kind. They also can usually only be found in fairly isolated or abandoned locations, so they don't affect environments at a large scale. Their toxicity also leads them to be avoided by most creatures.

Relationship with Humans

Humans tend to see Slimes as nothing more than pests, in the case of Toxic Slimes, due to their overall rarity, humans on average interact with them frequently. Though adventurers, hunters or explorers may find them in the wild.

In recent times people have begun to use the mucus their bodies produce in fertilizers, while the one from older individuals can serve as a potent cleaning agent, capable of easily removing rust thanks to its acidity. This makes it very valuable for metalworkers or warriors, who used it during maintenance of their gear.

The Research Institute of Pelagia has been using elemental cores from slimes, including Toxic Slimes, to power machines and automatons.
