Aqua Slime

One of the many kinds of slime found across Drakma, known for their affinity to the Aqua element.

Species Information

Type Elemental Lifeform
Diet Elemental Energy / Lay Line Residue
Habitat Wet areas rich in the water element
Average Size Small(40cm) to Large(3.15m)
Temperament Mostly passive / Reactive
Element Aqua
Danger Level Class I-II

Overview

Aqua Slimes are one of the most common variants of slime across Drakma, like all slimes they don't possess any sensory organs, or organs in general. They visualize the world through the lay lines, only reacting to external stimulus, most of the time they simply roam around areas rich in the water element. Their bodies are very slippery and gelatinous, even when compared to other slimes.

They are quite common near regions that have many rivers or lakes, or in places where humidity is high and rains frequent. Like other slimes, if they become old enough they can evolve into a more advanced elemental lifeform, or split into smaller ones.

Appearance

Their bodies are made of what appears to be water, but in a state where it looks gelatinous and slippery, like some sort of gel/slimy substance. This mass can look transparent, or carry the colors of the bodies of water they are found on, they also emit a bluish glow, especially near their core in the form of tendrils.

Their elemental core is located deep within their gelatinous bodies, it looks like and orb glowing with blue energy, the core although more solid than the rest of their bodies is still quite gelatinous and slick.

Aqua slimes can grow as they absorb more energy, they have the highest ceiling for growth when compared to other slimes. Though, due to the almost liquid consistency of their bodies, the bigger they get the harder it becomes for them to maintain their form on land.

Nature

These slimes spend the vast majority of their time in the water, sometimes it becomes hard to distinguish them from the puddle or water mass they are hiding on. Their bodies are quite soft, so physical attacks do little damage.

They can absorb water from their surroundings to use in pressurized jets to attack foes. They are quite vulnerable to the ice element, this is due to their bodies being composed of so much water.

Abilities

They possess many of the abilities other slimes have, but there are some unique ones too:

  • Absorption - They can absorb massive amounts of water from their surroundings to use during battle and to regenerate.
  • Gelatinous Mass: Their bodies are extremely soft, making physical attacks almost useless against them. They are also capable of regenerating over time.
  • Pressurized Blasts: Can release absorbed water in the form of powerful blasts of water, these can even damage stone.

Behavior

All slimes are pretty basic lifeforms, since they don't have sensory organs or follow the rules of biology they tend to look alien to some. Most of the time they just roam around, slowly sliding across the ground while occasionally feeding from the lay lines.

They only react to external stimuli, seeing the world through the information recorded within the lay lines. So if someone or something touches or hits them, they will not really feel it, they simply sense something and react according to the type of stimuli.

In the case of aqua slimes they tend to blast jets of water if they detect something they see as a threat. If left alone they usually simply ignore other creatures or people, remaining mostly motionless, some can even be confused for puddles.

Temporary Growth

Aqua Slimes can temporarily absorb great amounts of water from their surroundings to bust their size, larger individuals can store more water. Once enough is absorbed they become like huge water balloons, if attacked in this state they can end up releasing all of it in a big wave or blast of water.

Habitat

Aqua slimes can be found living in wet regions, rivers, lakes or even the ocean, when underwater they become extremely difficult to locate, as their bodies almost merge with the surround water. Occasionally they can spawn in other locations if there is an imbalance in the lay lines.

Ecology

Slimes are the base of the ecology for elemental lifeforms, being the weakest and most primitive, but also the starting point for most others elementals.

Due to their common habitats, Aqua Slimes are one of the most common kinds of slime, often causing trouble to humans, though mostly in harmless ways. Some creatures and plants, especially magical ones have learned to use these slimes to find sources of water during droughts, or even use the slimes themselves as water reserves.

Relationship with Humans

Humans tend to see Slimes as nothing more than pests, and Magma Slimes are no different, during the dry season in many parts of the continent these creatures cause wildfires that spread for miles.

The Hunters Guild keeps track of their populations and spawning grounds, making sure to eliminating them if their numbers increase too much. In recent times the Research Institute of Pelagia has been using elemental cores from slimes, including Aqua Slimes, to power machines and automatons.
