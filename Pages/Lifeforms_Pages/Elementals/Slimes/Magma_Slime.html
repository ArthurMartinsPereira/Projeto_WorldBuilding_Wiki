Magma Slime

One of the many kinds of slime found across Drakma, known for their affinity to the Ignis element.

Species Information

Type Elemental Lifeform
Diet Elemental Energy / Lay Line Residue

Overview

Magma Slimes are a relatively common variant of slime, like all slimes their bodies are amorphous blobs of elemental energy, they don't possess any sensory organs, or just organs in general. They visualize the world through the lay lines, only reacting to external stimulus, most of the time they simply roam around areas rich in the fire element.

They are quite common near volcanic regions, deep in the underground or near certain dragon layers. Like other slimes, if they become old enough they can evolve into a more advanced elemental lifeform, or split into smaller ones.

Appearance

Their bodies are made of what appears to be magma, but is actually a liquid/slime form of the fire element, they always glow in orange hues, but if cooled down they become darker.

Their elemental core is located deep within their gelatinous bodies, it looks like and orb glowing almost white-hot, the core is the only part of their bodies that is solid, though it still seems a bit gelatinous.

Magma slimes can grow as they absorb more energy, though there is a limit where they usually stop, growing too big makes moving difficult, and they can become unstable and explode.

Nature

These slimes have a nasty habit of exploding when their cores are removed or destroyed. Due to how hot their bodies are many regular metal weapons can melt if exposed to them for too long.

When they reach the end of their lifespan these slimes either split into smaller ones or evolve into an elemental construct of some kind.

Abilities

They possess many of the abilities other slimes have, but there are some changes:

  • Self Destruct - If their cores are removed or destroyed they explode. Radius and strength varies according to size.
  • Gelatinous Mass: Although more firm than other slimes, their bodies can still regenerate, making slashes and blunt attacks ineffective.
  • Accelerated Growth: They can increase their sizes rapidly by absorbing elemental energy from their surroundings, but this renders them unstable and soon end up releasing the accumulated power or exploding.

Behavior

All slimes are pretty basic lifeforms, since they don't have sensory organs or follow the rules of biology they tend to look alien to some. Most of the time they just roam around, slowly sliding across the ground while occasionally feeding from the lay lines.

They only react to external stimuli, seeing the world through the information recorded within the lay lines. So if someone or something touches or hits them, they will not really feel it, they simply sense something and react according to the type of stimuli.

In the case of magma slimes they tend to blast fire or fumes if they detect something they see as a threat. If left alone they usually simply ignore other creatures or people, only accidentally bumping into things.

Temporary Fusion

Magma Slimes can temporarily fuse with others of their kind to form a larger body for protection, similar to Aqua Slimes. But due to their elemental makeup these are highly unstable and more often than not end up with them exploding and scattering around. These explosions can be quite destructive if enough slimes fuse.

Habitat

Magma slimes can be found living in volcanic regions, deep underground caverns, or really any place that has geothermal activity or a high concentration of the fire element. Dragon layers can also be included if the dragon possess the fire element. Occasionally they can spawn in other locations if there is an imbalance in the lay lines.

Ecology

Slimes are the base of the ecology for elemental lifeforms, being the weakest and most primitive, but also the starting point for most others elementals.

Due to their isolated or dangerous habitats, Magma Slimes rarely make much of a difference in them. But if for some reason they appear in an unsuited environment such as a forest they can cause a lot of problems, especially during the dry season.

Relationship with Humans

Humans tend to see Slimes as nothing more than pests, and Magma Slimes are no different, during the dry season in many parts of the continent these creatures cause wildfires that spread for miles.

The Hunters Guild keeps track of their populations and spawning grounds, making sure to eliminating them if their numbers increase too much. In recent times the Research Institute of Pelagia has been using elemental cores from slimes, including Magma Slimes, to power machines and automatons.
