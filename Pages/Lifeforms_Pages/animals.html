The Fauna of Drakma

Catalog of the known notable animal species found across Drakma

Drakes

One of the most common families of creatures in Drakma, there are many species of drakes all across the continent, from the mighty Jungle Tirants to the small House Drakes, they have filled almost every niche available. Common caracteristics among drakes include, being repitile like and possessing a kind of natural armor plating or shell over their bodies.

Wyrms

A family of creatures distantly related to the drakes, Wyrms possess long, almost serpentine bodies,

Wyverns

A family of flying repitile creatures, known for being able to condensate elemental energy in a kind of vapor on special organs, when released they form clouds of a hazardous magic gas carrying the elemental energy.

DireWolfs

A species of wolf found in most of the north and central regions of Drakma, they are much larger than regular wolfs and can even control certain elements like, wind and ice.
