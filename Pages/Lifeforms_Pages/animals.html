The Fauna of Drakma

Catalog of the known notable animal species found across Drakma

Drakes

One of the most common families of creatures in Drakma, there are many species of drakes all across the continent, from the mighty Jungle Tirants to the small House Drakes, they have filled almost every niche available. Common caracteristics among drakes include, being repitile like and possessing a kind of natural armor plating or shell over their bodies.

Wyrms

A family of creatures distantly related to the drakes, Wyrms possess long, serpentine bodies, only their fore limbs are developed, their back legs are very underdeveloped and almost non-existent. Wyrms usualy live in the underground digging tunels or nestign within deep caverns, they can recover from almost all wounds, and can grow in to adulthood extremely fast as long as there is food available.

Wyverns

A family of flying repitile creatures, known for being able to condensate elemental energy in a kind of vapor on special organs, when released they form clouds of a hazardous magic gas carrying the elemental energy. Like drakes and wyrms, there are many different species of Wyverns, varying in size, shappe and abilities.

DireWolfs

A species of wolf found in most of the north and central regions of Drakma, they are much larger than regular wolfs and can even control certain elements like, wind and ice. In the far north they form packs with other Dire Wolfs, but in southern regions due to them being much rarer they can be found leading packs of regular wolfs.
