Overview

Sea Weavers are a small species of fish that inhabit various coral reefs across Drakma, they are known for forming large colonies and build nests into corals or with algae. They have the unique ability to produce a biding agent that can harden fast even underwater, they use this substance to create large nests within coral reefs or by weaving algae together. In a way they are basically underwater bees.

Appearance

Sea Weavers are small fishes with pointy heads, their colonies are divided in casts, so depending on the cast their appearances may change, the few qualities all casts share is the reinforced scales on their heads and their "beaks". Architects are the smallest and most proliferous in the colonies, their "beaks" are used almost as syringes to expel the biding agent they use to build repair and reinforce the colony.

Workers are slightly larger than the Architects, they are responsible for gathering and transporting food and resources across the colony, in rare situations workers can transition into higher casts if needed. They don't produce the same binding agent as Architects, but they are capable of producing a corrosive substance, they use it to help conserve food, but it can be used for defense too.

Soldiers are slightly larger than the workers, the thicker scales on their heads cover more of their bodies, their beaks are used as a weapon, with them ramming into targets and injecting a cocktail of toxins that paralyze their targets. Soldiers usually work in small groups, but can send chemical signals for backup when needed.

Royals are the most important members of a colony, usually being composed of a dominant male and female. They are much larger and have a greater control over elemental energy. They act as the king and queen of the colony, with most other weavers being their children. The queens are the largest and most long-lived ones, while kings are faster and better combatants, they act as the last line of defense for the queen, it's possible to for a colony to have multiple kings, though one still acts as the dominant one.

Behavior

In a way, Sea Weavers act very similarly to bees, though their colonies division is a bit more complex and their set of abilities more varied.

Workers spend the majority or their time collecting and storing food, they are also the ones responsible for producing a special kind of jelly/Honney that is used to feed the queen and her young.

Soldiers guard the outside of the colonies structure and also patrol certain chambers, they will swarm and attack any creature that is not recognize as part of the colony. Their stings don't do much damage but the neurotoxin they inject can kill small foes if they get swarmed.

The queen usually remains in the central chamber, being guarded by the king and other large soldiers. She can move around and will occasionally move to the brood chamber to lay eggs. The king will follow and them fertilize the eggs.

It is possible for other members of the colony to reproduce, but those born from the workers and soldiers are unable to change casts or develop into new monarchs.

Sometimes a new queen or king may be born, these will remain in the colony for some time, but usually they soon leave with some workers to build a new colony or find a mate.

Sea Weavers usually don't fight against others of their kind, but some species have developed more parasitic or aggressive behaviors. Either raiding or invading other colonies.

Habitat

Sea Weavers are known for inhabiting areas rich in coral reefs or with specific species of algae, these are essential for them to build up their colonies, mani species of Sea Weaver even have Symbiotic relationships with these.

Colonies can grow over time, the weavers also help to protect and care for the corals or algae they use, keeping them healthy and protecting against parasites and other creatures that might harm them.

Abandoned colonies often become lairs for various kinds of creatures that inhabit the sea, especially small species of fish and invertebrates.
