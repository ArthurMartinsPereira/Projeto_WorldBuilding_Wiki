Overview

Puppeteer Ants are one of the most exotic and strange species found in the Wildlands, these insects live in massive colonies and are divided among various different casts, some ants are as small as a coin while others as small mammals. The queens can grow to reach a size similar to that of a dog over time. They are especially infamous for their ability to build colonies by using the corpses of dead creatures, they use silk and their own bodies to be able to somewhat repair and move the colony around. They might even imitate simple actions, in low visibility they can easily trick prey or predators to approach them before attacking.

Appearance

Sea Weavers are small fishes with pointy heads, their colonies are divided in casts, so depending on the cast their appearances may change, the few qualities all casts share is the reinforced scales on their heads and their "beaks". Workers are the smallest and most proliferous in the colonies, their "beaks" are used almost as syringes to expel the biding agent they use to build repair and reinforce the colony.

Abilities

  • Tail Whip: Their enormous tails can be swung with tremendous force. The metallic scales at the tip form a flexible cutting edge capable of causing devastating injuries.
  • Geo Armor: Jungle Tyrants can channel Geo energy through their mineralized scales, temporarily reinforcing their natural armor and making them significantly more resistant to physical attacks.
  • Animus Enhancement: Animus energy can be circulated through their bodies to enhance their already immense physical strength and endurance.
  • Elemental Shockwave: By stomping the ground and releasing elemental energy through their limbs, Jungle Tyrants can create powerful shockwaves capable of knocking nearby creatures off their feet.
  • Titanic Strength: Jungle Tyrants possess extraordinary physical strength and are considered one of the strongest naturally occurring species in Drakma.

Trivia

  • Jungle Tyrants are featured on the banner of the Forest Watch and are considered one of the symbols of Falkore.
  • Some of the oldest Jungle Tyrant nests are large enough to be mistaken for natural hills from a distance.
  • Jungle Tyrant migration routes have been used as roads by Falkorean communities for centuries.
