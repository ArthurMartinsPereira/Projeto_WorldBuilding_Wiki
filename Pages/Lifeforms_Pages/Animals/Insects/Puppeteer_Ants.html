Overview

Puppeteer Ants are one of the most exotic and strange species found in the Wildlands, these insects live in massive colonies and are divided among various different casts, some ants are as small as a coin while others as small mammals. The queens can grow to reach a size similar to that of a dog over time. They are especially infamous for their ability to build colonies by using the corpses of dead creatures, they use silk and their own bodies to be able to somewhat repair and move the colony around. They might even imitate simple actions, in low visibility they can easily trick prey or predators to approach them before attacking.

Appearance

The appearance of Puppeteer Ants varies according to their cast, their size also varies a lot, with workers being just a fraction of the size of the queen and her guards. The structure of their colonies are extremely complex, with various sub-casts existing to perform single specific tasks.

Workers are divided among a few different casts, some responsible for collecting and storing food, managing the queen's brood, repairing, shaping and moving the colony around. Some are even used only to send chemical signals across the colony or to act as "eyes" for the colony.

The soldiers tend to be many times larger than Workers, depending on the cast some might even bee as large as a cat. Though these usually don't move around much and remain in certain parts of the colony body, to provide defense. The largest of their kind are capable of spiting an acidic substance against enemies.

The Queen is by far the largest member of the colony, being able to grow to the size of a large dog, though on this stage she also becomes completely incapable of moving by herself. Requiring the help of workers whose she commands through the use of pheromones, the queen is also capable of attacking when necessary, though usually this only happens when things get desperate.

Behavior

The colonies of these creatures vary greatly in size, but generally the older the colony the larger it gets, though there is a limit. As colonies grow they accumulate the bones and parts of more creatures, increasing their size and the complexity of their mimicking abilities.

Puppeteer Ants tend to prey on anything they can get, be them prey, other predators, even humans are not safe. Their main hunting strategy is to use the bones and carcases they collect to create puppets with roughly the same proportions and silhouette of an animal, they may even mimic simple behaviors and movements. They then will spread the rest of the colony around the surrounding area, and if some creature gets withing reach, the entire thing launches at them to consume them.

The larger the colony the more complex their mimicking is, and the large the area they can cover. In the misty jungles where they live it's hard for most creatures to identify that they silhouette they see is a trick. But as colonies grow they also become more voracious and aggressive, even attacking other colonies.

The largest colonies may look like hills covered in bones, these tend to be extremely dangerous to approach, despite their size they can be deceptively agile, being able to extend parts of the colony to reach targets in an instant. The bones they collect are used to create a firm structure for the colony, letting the ants pile up and organize the structure using their own bodies and silk to simulate tendons and muscles.

Habitat

This specie is native to the Fossilized Forest in the Wildlands, they can't be found anywhere else and tend to remain in the warmer areas of their habitat.

Their presence can shape their environments in various ways, from leaving behind piles of older bones and carcases to even creating clear paths through the dense jungle as they move.

Older colonies are particularly known for creating clearings in certain areas once they become less mobile.

Life Cycle

The life span of these ants vary according to cast, the queen may live for decades, while the workers live for at most 6 months.

After the queen lays an agg it is stored in another part of the colony, they are protected and cared for by the workers, once they hatch the young spend another week or two being cared for by the workers. Depending on the cast some might take longer to fully grow and develop, but usually by this time they can at least walk by themselves.

Soldiers usually take longer to fully mature, especially the specialized casts that grow to become huge. Some soldiers also leave the colony during the reproduction season.

Queens take a long time to fully mature, though for them to reach their maximum size it might take many years, and it only happens once the colony reaches a certain mass.
