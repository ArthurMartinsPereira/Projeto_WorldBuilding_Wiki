Overview

The Fortress Termites are and endemic species of the Wildlands, they are known for their unique symbiotic relationship with the Petra Flora that can be found in the central regions of the Wildlands. These insects can inhabit the large Petra Wood Trees and fortify them over time, turning them into real fortresses against other species of insects and small animals.

Appearance

There are three main castes in a colony of Fortress Termites, the Workers who are responsible for building, repairing, collecting and storing food. Soldiers, responsible for guarding the colony against external threats and protecting workers when they are outside. The Royals, simply responsible for reproducing, the Queen is the most important in this group, with the Kings having fairly short lifespans compared to their mates, living for at most a few months, for this reason there are usually multiple of them around.

In appearance, Fortress Termites look a bitt larger than the common termites, their bodies also seem a bit sturdier and better protected. The Kings and Queens are the largest ones, after their first mating, the Queen's abdomen begins to grow and swell becoming multiple times her size, after this they become mostly immobile, being care for by the workers and Kings.

During the nuptial flight, both young kings and queens have wings, but after mating and forming a new colony they lose them and remain grounded.

Soldiers are a bit larger than workers, having pronounced mandibles and a darker coloration around their bodies, they are also more armored, with a thicker exoskeleton to protect them.

Behavior

Fortress Termites are mostly known for their massive, fortified colonies, build inside the Petra Wood trees. These large trees naturally grow a mineralized bark around their trunks, the Termites use a special substance their produce to melt and reinforce the fissures and entrances around the tree.

During the harsh winters of the Wildlands, the bark of these trees contract and become almost completely sealed. This helps to keep the termites, and other creatures that use them as shelter, safe from the harsh whether.

Though due to this contraction, many of the fortifications made by the termites are damaged or destroyed, forcing them to start rebuilding when spring finally arrives and the tree opens up again.

Fortress Termites are not really parasitic in nature, they put a lot of effort in defending the tree they make home and usually never move their colony from them, even if the tree dies.

This makes them very protective of their homes, they won't take any chances, so if they notice other insects or creatures that could represent a danger to their tree they will attack.

They use the trees to also produce their own food source in the form of a special fungi, they carve chambers in parts of the tree and use them as farms. They tend to make sure the fungi don't grow too much or harm the tree, but if the colony becomes weakened its growth may go out of control.

Colonies only move from one tree to another in extremely rare cases, usually if the tree dies the colony goes with it after some time, especially when inter arrives and the cold is able to seep inside the colony.

Habitat

These termites are endemic to the central regions of the Wildlands, also known as the Fossilized Forest due to its petrawood trees.

Under the care of the colonies, the tree they inhabit tend to grow to become truly massive, this makes it easy to figure out with ones are inhabited by large colonies.

There are a few animals that occasionally prey on the termites, such as birds, rodents and other insects. Even the Puppeteer Ants have occasionally been observed raiding colonies of Fortress Termites.

Life Cycle

Fortress Termite colonies can survive for decades, even if the original queen dies she can be replaced. A colony only really dies when no replacement queen can be found or if their home tree dies.

Some of the oldest recorded colonies have been dated to be over a hundred years, and they still inhabit the same trees, caring for them and protect them against threats.

The oldest colonies tend to reinforce their homes so much that eventually some trees end up looking like they are being swallowed by a hill.

Abilities

  • Reinforcement Fluid: They can produce a kind of natural cement that they use to build and reinforce the structure of the colony.
  • Specialization: Aside from the main castes there are subdivisions within them, these are composed of highly specialized termites.
  • Swarm Tactics: If a soldier detects danger it can release a chemical cocktail of pheromones that alert the colony, after this more soldier with apear to help deal with the enemy.
  • Elemental Manipulation: Extremely limited, but still present, they can generate small doses of geo energy to help in the hardening process of their cement.
  • Corrosive Fluid: Some workers can produce a highly corrosive substance that can melt through the mineral bark of the trees they inhabit, they use this to carve tunnels or to clear obstructions. Can be used in suicide attacks to enemies.

Ecology

Fortress Termites are quite important to the forests of the wild lands, partly due to forming the basis of the food chain for many animals, but especially for the trees they inhabit.

Petrawood trees that are inhabited by them tend to be healthier and grow to become much larger and prosperous when compared to others without a colony. The termites can even help to spread the seeds of these trees acros the forest.

These termites also tend to reuse the minerals left behind by fallen branches and pieces of bark, that way they don't have to take resources from the tree and end up harming it.

Some subspecies of Fortress termites also developed the habit of colonizing smaller vegetation, in some cases even building their colonies under the roots of the plant instead of incide or on top like most do.

Relationship with Humans

The human population of the Wildlands is mainly composed of nomadic tribes, so due they aren't known for building many permanent structures. Their main habitations are tents that they developed to be more easily disassembled and assembled.

But for generations some ancient, hollowed out, colonies of Fortress Termites have also been used as shelter by them, many of these have been in use for centuries already.

The nomad tribes also learned how to use crushed pieces of these colonies to create a special claw they use for pottery, writing tablets and forging. Due to its durability these items are very valued by them.

Some artisans in these tribes even developed secret techniques to make blades and armor with this clay, though this art is a closely kept secret among the tribes that possess it.
