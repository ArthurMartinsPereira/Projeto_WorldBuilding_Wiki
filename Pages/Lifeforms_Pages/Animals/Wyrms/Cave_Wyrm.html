Overview

Cavern Wyrms are large serpentine reptiles that inhabit the depths of Drakma, they dig vast tunnel networks that connect the underground and most of the time don't leave their territories. They are solitary creatures, only searching for others during mating season, or early in their lives when they are small and live in colonies of hatchlings. Because of their adaptability and insatiable appetite, Cave Wyrms are considered one of the most widespread subterranean predators in Drakma.

Appearance

Wyrms in general all have long sinuous bodies, only the forelimbs remain fully developed, their hind legs have become little more than vestigial stubs. Cavern Wyrms are one of the largest species of Wyrm, being able to grow to over 8 meters in length and 2.5 meters in height, they possess thick scales that protect their bodies from the sharp rocks of the underground. Cave Wyrms are usually found in shades of gray, black, brown and dark green.

Behavior

The behavior of Wyrms change as they age, when they are born their parents immediately abandon the nest, leaving the hatchlings to fend for themselves. Due to their extremely fast metabolism, Wyrms are almost constantly hungry, so these newborns dig in mass to the surface to avoid adults and find food, they can devastate crop fields and eventually start attacking livestock. Luckily this only happens once a year, by the end of mating season.

Wyrms don't grow at a normal pace like other creatures, the more food they have available the faster they grow into adulthood. They can eat almost anything, even carrion and inorganic matter. Initially the young may form colonies for protection, but as they age they go back to the underground to establish their own territories.

Adult Cave Wyrms are extremely territorial, they attack anything that they consider a threat or a rival. Their roars work both as a rudimentary echolocation and a sonic weapon, one so strong that ancient Wyrms are usually deaf, having to rely solely on their powerful sense of smell to locate prey and others of their kind.

Habitat

As their name suggests, they are mostly found underground, living in large cave systems and tunnels they dig, it's rare to find adults on the surface, but not unheard of. They tend to feed on small critters, underground flora, carrion and even others of their kind, there is nothing they won't eat to try and satiate their vicious appetites. They tend to avoid areas where water frequently seeps in, they are terrible swimmers.

During hatching season, thousands of Hatchlings come to the surface in droves, devouring anything they can find. To keep their population under control and minimize the damage they pose the Hunters guild often performs exterminations on colonies. This is especially used as training for recruits since young wyrms do not pose much danger.

Abilities

  • Rapid Regeneration: Can recover from near fatal injuries within days.
  • Sonic Scream: Produces a focused roar capable of stunning prey.
  • High Physical Strength: Powerful tail and forelimbs, perfect for digging.
  • Rarely Elemental Abilities(Ignis/Geo): Rarely may manifest limited elemental abilities.

Ecology

Although nowadays, their presence is mostly considered similar to pests and other infestations, in the past they weren't such a big problem for nature, there were various predators that would hunt them. But due to human activity this number declined, which gave wyrms the opportunity to proliferate. Nowadays, the Guild is required to keep their population in check.

Adult Wyrms are notoriously difficult to kill, not because of their strength, but due to their ability to survive near fatal injuries. Their bodies can regenerate very quickly from wounds and stop heavy bleeding in minutes. Their scale armor is mostly made of their old dead skin and scales, forming dense layers that protect them from any attacks unable to pierce through them.

Relationship with Humans

Although there are a few species of wyrm that have been domesticated by humans, this isn't one of them, unfortunately. Aside from their dense scales there is nothing in the Cave Wyrms that can be used, no elemental organ to be extracted, their flesh is bitter, hard and often poisonous, their smell tends to also be pretty strong. For these and other reason humans for the most part just hate this kind of wyrm.

Trivia

  • Cave Wyrms never stop growing if enough food is available.
  • Ancient individuals eventually become deaf due to the power of their own sonic roars.
  • Hatchlings are one of the most common first hunts assigned to Hunters Guild recruits.
  • Their tunnel systems can remain stable for centuries after being abandoned.
  • Some abandoned Wyrm tunnels eventually become underground roads used by explorers.
