Overview

Cavern Wyrms are large serpentine reptiles that inhabit the depths of Drakma, they dig vast tunnel networks that connect the underground and most of the time don't leave their territories. They are solitary creatures, only searching for others during mating season, or early in their lives when they are small and live in colonies of hatchlings.

Appearance

Wyrms in general all have long sinuous bodies, only their forelimbs are still developed, their back legs have long devolved into small stubs. Cave Wyrms are one of the largest species of Wyrm, being able to grow to over 8 meters in length and 2.5 in height, they possess thick scales that protect their bodies from the sharp rocks of the underground. Cave Wyrms usually come in darker shades of color, such as grays, black, brown and deep greens.

Behavior

The behavior of Wyrms change as they age, when they are born their parents immediately abandon the nest, leaving the hatchlings to fend for themselves. Due to their metabolism Wyrms are almost always hungry, so these newborns dig in mass to the surface to avoid adults and find food, they can devastate crop fields and eventually start attacking livestock.

Wyrms don't grow at a normal pace like other creatures, the more food they have available the faster they grow into adulthood. Initially the young may form colonies for protection, but as they age they go back to the underground to establish their own territories.

Adult Cave Wyrms are extremely territorial, they attack anything that they consider a threat or a rival. Their roars work both as a rudimentary echolocation and a sonic weapon, one so strong that ancient Wyrms are usually deaf, having to rely souly on their powerful sense of smell to locate prey and others of their kind.

Habitat

As their name suggests, they are mostly found underground, living in large cave systems and tunnels they dig, it's rare to find adults on the surface, but not unheard of. They tend to feed on small critters, underground flora, carrion and even others of their kind, there is nothing they won't eat to try and satiate their vicious apetites. They tend to avoid areas where water frequently seeps in, they are terrible swimmers.

During hatching season, thousands of Hatchlings come to the surface in droves, devouring anything they can find. To keep their population under control and minimize the damage they pose the Hunters guild often performs exterminations on colonies. This is especially used as training for recruits since young wyrms do not pose much danger.

Abilities

  • Rapid Regeneration
  • Sonic Scream
  • High Physical Strength
  • Rarely Elemental Abilities(Ignis/Geo)

Ecology

Although now days their presence is mostly considered similar to pests and other infestations, in the past they weren't such a big problem for nature, there were various predators that would hunt them. But due to human activity this number declined, what gave wyrms the chance to proliferate. Now days the Guild is required to keep their population in check.

Relationship with Humans

Although there are a few species of wyrm that have been domesticated by humans, this isn't one of them
