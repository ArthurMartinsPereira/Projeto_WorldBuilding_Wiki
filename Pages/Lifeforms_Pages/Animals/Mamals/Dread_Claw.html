Overview

Dire Claws are one of the largest predatory species in Drakma, they look like enormous bears, with their most pronounced characteristic being the huge retractable claws they are known for. They mostly inhabit colder regions, due to their size and fur they have a hard time surviving in warmer climates. The common Dire Claws are divided into two subspecies for the most part, the Southern Dread Claw and Northern Dread Claw. This distinction mostly separates the ones living north of the Crown of Fangs mountains from the ones on the south.

Appearance

For the most part Dread Claws are very similar to bears, having the same body shape and bone structure, but they tend to be much larger and physically stronger. Their two main features are their signature claws, which are quite long, sharp and resistant, and their special fur, which is adapted to grow a unique moss that both masks their scent and can be used for camouflage.

Northern and southern Dread Claws are very similar, the actual difference is the color of their fur and moss. Southern ones tend to have lighter shades of brown and their moss comes in shades of light green. Northern ones have darker coats and their moss comes in colors that help them hide among the tundra or pine forests of the region.

Behavior

Dread Claws tend to be quite territorial when facing others of their species or creatures they see as a threat, but in general they are quite passive to small animals as long as they are not hungry or hurt. Displaying their claws without a direct attack is usually a warning sign to back off.

These creatures, like regular bears, will consume lots of calories in preparation for winter, ten they will look for caves and other places where they can remain safe from whether. During this period they will only attack if directly provoked or threatened, many small animals use this as an advantage, following them into their lairs to remain safe.

During hunts, they prefer to ambush their prey, they are not very fast so their best chances are with surprise attacks. Thanks to their moss coverage, prey has a hard time sensing their smell or seeing them, allowing them to be quite successful hunters. If they are unable to hunt they can also consume berries, roots, nuts or other vegetation they may find.

Dread claws mark their territory with both their scent, which is only felt by others of their kind, and by carving marks on tress and rocks with their claws.

Habitat

Dread Claws prefer to inhabit cold, or at least temperate forests and regions. They can go into open areas to hunt prey but usually prefer forested environments if they are sleeping or resting. Their preferred prey are the Northern Bison, moose, deer and wild hogs, they also like fish if available to them.

Adults claim large territories for themselves, males are very protective of their lands, but they may allow a female they are interested in to stay for some time. They don't kill cubs unless they are already in a state of aggression.

Abilities

  • Physical Amplification
  • Breath Attack
  • Concealment
  • Pack Enhancement

Ecology

Although in the past they were seem as dangerous, due to them avoiding humans, they weren't much of a threat. But now days, due to humans encroaching on their natural habitats, Dire Wolves have begun to act far more cunningly and aggressively. But in general they don't actually cause a unbalance in the ecosystems they inhabit.

Relationship with Humans

Due to the recent increase in attacks, many human settlements, especially those on the countryside have begun to fear that their livestock or people might be attacked by packs of wolves, caravans also have reported attacks. For this reason the Hunters Guild has been trying their best to either relocate or eliminate dangerous packs. Their fur is very valuable as a material for magical cloaks and armor, their fangs and claws can be used in alchemy too, but mostly as replacements for rarer ingredients.
