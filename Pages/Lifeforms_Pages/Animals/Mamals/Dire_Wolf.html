Overview

Dire Wolves are a large subspecies of wolf that inhabits much of the central and northern regions of Drakma. They are known for their cunning and strength, often they act as pack leaders for common wolves, leading them into hunts and using their control over wind and ice to hide and perform surprise attacks.

Appearance

In general, they look like very large wolves, their fangs are more elongated than normal and the color of their fur varies from region to region, from browns, grays, blacks and whites. Their fur also has strange patterns of a lighter shade, they almost seem to form channels over their bodies, when Dire Wolves use their elemental powers these markings light up.

Behavior

Dire Wolves are extremely smart when compared to common breeds of wolf, they rarely engage enemies they can't take down and often prepare ambushes, they seem to have a good memory and have shown to remember individuals. In nature most of them can be found leading packs of regular wolves, or rarely, packs of Dire Wolves lead by stronger Alpha.

In general, they seem to be very competent leaders, considering most packs lead by one grow to become far larger than regular ones. Although they live among lesser wolves they only breed with others of their kind, making hybrids extremely rare.

Habitat

As their name suggests, they are mostly found underground, living in large cave systems and tunnels they dig, it's rare to find adults on the surface, but not unheard of. They tend to feed on small critters, underground flora, carrion and even others of their kind, there is nothing they won't eat to try and satiate their vicious apetites. They tend to avoid areas where water frequently seeps in, they are terrible swimmers.

During hatching season, thousands of Hatchlings come to the surface in droves, devouring anything they can find. To keep their population under control and minimize the damage they pose the Hunters guild often performs exterminations on colonies. This is especially used as training for recruits since young wyrms do not pose much danger.

Abilities

  • Rapid Regeneration
  • Sonic Scream
  • High Physical Strength
  • Rarely Elemental Abilities(Ignis/Geo)

Ecology

Although now days their presence is mostly considered similar to pests and other infestations, in the past they weren't such a big problem for nature, there were various predators that would hunt them. But due to human activity this number declined, what gave wyrms the chance to proliferate. Now days the Guild is required to keep their population in check.

Relationship with Humans

Although there are a few species of wyrm that have been domesticated by humans, this isn't one of them, unfortunately. Aside from their dense scales there is nothing in the Cave Wyrms that can be used, no elemental organ to be extracted, their flesh is biter, hard and often poisonous, their smell tends to also be pretty strong. For these and other reason humans for the most part just hate this kind of wyrm.
