A large subspecies of wolf that can channel elemental energy.

Overview

Dire Wolves are a large subspecies of wolf that inhabits much of the central and northern regions of Drakma. They are known for their cunning and strength, often they act as pack leaders for common wolves, leading them into hunts and using their control over wind and ice to hide and perform surprise attacks.

Appearance

In general, they look like very large wolves, their fangs are more elongated than normal and the color of their fur varies from region to region, from browns, grays, blacks and whites. Their fur also has strange patterns of a lighter shade, they almost seem to form channels over their bodies, when Dire Wolves use their elemental powers these markings light up.

Behavior

Dire Wolves are extremely smart when compared to common breeds of wolf, they rarely engage enemies they can't take down and often prepare ambushes, they seem to have a good memory and have shown to remember individuals. In nature most of them can be found leading packs of regular wolves, or rarely, packs of Dire Wolves lead by stronger Alpha.

In general, they seem to be very competent leaders, considering most packs lead by one grow to become far larger than regular ones. Although they live among lesser wolves they only breed with others of their kind, making hybrids extremely rare.

Dire Wolves usually claim large territories for themselves and their packs, many times forcing smaller ones to merge with theirs or be expelled from those lands.

Habitat

This species usually prefers regions with a colder climate, but can also live in temperate ones. Although they can be seen hunting with their packs in plains, they generally make their dems in deep forest where they can hide more easily.

Due to human expansion, many of the areas this specie inhabits have begun to shrink, this has caused them to come into conflict with humans far more often. Some even have seemingly started to develop strategies to avoid hunters and attack live stock.

Abilities

  • Physical Amplification
  • Breath Attack
  • Concealment
  • Pack Enhancement

Ecology

Although in the past they were seem as dangerous, due to them avoiding humans, they weren't much of a threat. But now days, due to humans encroaching on their natural habitats, Dire Wolves have begun to act far more cunningly and aggressively. But in general they don't actually cause a unbalance in the ecosystems they inhabit.

Relationship with Humans

Due to the recent increase in attacks, many human settlements, especially those on the countryside have begun to fear that their livestock or people might be attacked by packs of wolves, caravans also have reported attacks. For this reason the Hunters Guild has been trying their best to either relocate or eliminate dangerous packs. Their fur is very valuable as a material for magical cloaks and armor, their fangs and claws can be used in alchemy too, but mostly as replacements for rarer ingredients.
