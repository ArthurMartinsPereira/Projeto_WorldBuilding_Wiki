Overview

A mighty species of bird of prey that inhabits various regions of Falkore, being one of the largest species of birds around the continent. Although difficult, these creatures can be tamed, though it usually requires to taken them while still young.

Appearance

Falkorian Harpies have redish brown feathers covering their bodies, with a white underbelly and some black accents, they possess powerful talons that can easily cut through flesh or rook into the preys body. Atop of their head they have a crown of feathers, with two at the opposite ends being longer, when open they almost look like horns from afar. Females don't grow the two larger feathers on their crown.

Their beaks are a bit large and slightly serrated at some sections, the tip is pointy and hook shaped, being much tougher than the rest. The color of their beaks is a bright yellow, with the tip being a dark gray or black.

Their eyes provide them with an excellent sight, allowing them to find and follow targets from afar with extreme precision. Their tail feathers are long and the tips tend to be colored in black, followed by white and them the redish brown of their bodies.

Hatchlings are born with a fluffy cover of down feathers, these are usually gray or brown, to help with camouflage, as they age they will grow their permanent feathers and develop their talons and beak.

Behavior

Falkorian Harpies are known for being very territorial when they reach adulthood, they will claim swats of jungles as their territory and chase of any other flying creatures they consider a threat.

Until they find a mate they will hunt alone, males tend to begin building a nest as soon as they can, if a female becomes interested they will form a couple. From there on they will hunt together and protect each other, Harpies mate for life most of the time.

Once the female lays a clutch of eggs, they will take turns caring for them, after they hatch this behavior continues. Parents will care for their young for roughly a year, after that they either leave or are expelled from their nests.
