Mountain Drake

A large reptilian animal known for inhabit the mountains of central and northern Drakma.

Overview

Mountain Drakes are one of the most common, large drake species in Drakma. They can be found inhabiting much of the mountainous regions of the north and heartlands. These animals are especially known for their extremely resilient, mineral scale armor. They can often be found calmly sleeping in groups or roaming lonely across valleys between mountains. Normally they don't attack humans, not seeing them as real prey due to size.

Appearance

Mountain Drakes are large quadrupedal reptilians, like most drake species, they have a dense, mineralized, scale armor growing over their bodies, making them extremely resilient to damage. The color and appearance of the armor depends on the minerals consumed by the drake, the resistance of the armor is also influenced by this. Their scales are usually colored in green, reds, blues, grays and blacks. Their armors can make they look spiky, especially their tails, these also sometimes end up resembling large clubs.

Behavior

For the most part, Mountain Drakes don't attack small or medium-sized species, they prefer to hunt large prey, suc as the mountain bison, wyrms or even other drake species. Many small critters even began to associate them with safety, remaining close to these creatures for protection.

But this does not mean they are passive, if threatened of desperate they will viciously attack their target, this is especially devastating when they are in groups. These groups, of usually around 4 to 7 individuals are formed after mating season, they are temporary and tend to only last until they hatchlings grow old enough to accompany their mothers during travels.

Males tend to be larger and are especially aggressive if they are guarding eggs or young drakelings. In preparation for the mating season males make large nests using, branches, rocks and minerals. Usually the female that accepts them will eat the minerals, they help with the formations of the mineralized shells for their eggs.

Once the eggs hatch the young drakes will eat the shells to get some minerals from it, helping they develop their first armor, they remain with their parents for around a year.

Habitat

As their name suggests, they are mostly found underground, living in large cave systems and tunnels they dig, it's rare to find adults on the surface, but not unheard of. They tend to feed on small critters, underground flora, carrion and even others of their kind, there is nothing they won't eat to try and satiate their vicious appetites. They tend to avoid areas where water frequently seeps in, they are terrible swimmers.

During hatching season, thousands of Hatchlings come to the surface in droves, devouring anything they can find. To keep their population under control and minimize the damage they pose the Hunters guild often performs exterminations on colonies. This is especially used as training for recruits since young wyrms do not pose much danger.

Abilities

  • Rapid Regeneration: Can recover from near fatal injuries within days.
  • Sonic Scream: Produces a focused roar capable of stunning prey.
  • High Physical Strength: Powerful tail and forelimbs, perfect for digging.
  • Rarely Elemental Abilities(Ignis/Geo): Rarely may manifest limited elemental abilities.

Ecology

Although nowadays, their presence is mostly considered similar to pests and other infestations, in the past they weren't such a big problem for nature, there were various predators that would hunt them. But due to human activity this number declined, which gave wyrms the opportunity to proliferate. Nowadays, the Guild is required to keep their population in check.

Adult Wyrms are notoriously difficult to kill, not because of their strength, but due to their ability to survive near fatal injuries. Their bodies can regenerate very quickly from wounds and stop heavy bleeding in minutes. Their scale armor is mostly made of their old dead skin and scales, forming dense layers that protect them from any attacks unable to pierce through them.

Relationship with Humans

Although there are a few species of wyrm that have been domesticated by humans, this isn't one of them, unfortunately. Aside from their dense scales there is nothing in the Cave Wyrms that can be used, no elemental organ to be extracted, their flesh is bitter, hard and often poisonous, their smell tends to also be pretty strong. For these and other reason humans for the most part just hate this kind of wyrm.
