Overview

The mighty Jungle Tyrants of Falkore are considered one of the largest species of drake in Drakma, being the tallest among them. These giants are known for inhabiting the massive jungles of Falkore, although they are herbivores they are one of the most dangerous creatures in the nation when irritated. They often travel in herds lead by the oldest male or female in the group, these creatures are fiercely protective of their young. Some groups in Falkore developed techniques to train and domesticate Jungle Tyrants, though these are a close kept secret.

Appearance

Jungle Tyrants are huge sauropod looking drakes, their necks and tail are long, with the tail being almost twice the length of the body. Like other drakes, they also grow a mineralized scale armor for protection, though theirs, due to the lack of minerals in their habitats, are far more sparce, only covering their shoulders, spine, legs and the base of the neck. The tip of their tail has a specialized layer of metalic scales, they are flexible and extremely sharp, they can use their tail as a whip, easily being capable of slicing a person in two. They often sharp their tail blade against rocks or parts of their own armor. Their scales come in various tones of green and brow, their tail blades and armored bits have a darker metalic color. Males have special fins and air-sacks on their necks covered in bright colors, they use this to attract mates.

Behavior

Jungle Tyrants tend to be relatively calm and social, often traveling in small or large family units, these are lead by the oldest and most experienced male or female of the herd. They prefer to nest in areas that are high but also close to sources of fresh water. Occasionally herds migrate to different regions, this mostly happens during mating season, they migrate to areas abundant in fruits and foliage they feed off.

These drakes are protective of their young and will attack any other creatures that might approach them if they sense danger. This has lead to most other animals, including predators, to avoid them, especially if they have drakelings with them. Their nests are made with large rocks, branches, logs and even mud, being built to last, many have even been used for generations by the same herds. Their nests are comunal, the whole hear maintains them and cares for the young.

Males tend to be larger and are colored in brighter colors, they possess special fins and air-sacks on their necks that they use for display during mating season. Females generally choose those with the brightest colors and impressive fins. Some males even try to damage the fins of their peers to sabotage their chances. Both males and females take good care of their tail blades, being their greatest weapon.

It's rare to find a solitary Jungle Tyrant, these are either young drakes that have left their original herds, or pariahs. The pariahs tend to be males whose fins have gotten too damaged or are too aggressive and were expelled, these can be extremely dangerous due to the stress and loneliness.

Habitat

Jungle Tyrants are native to the jungles and forests or Falkore, they can't be found anywhere else. The prefer regions rich in dense vegetation and water,

Ecology

Mountain Drakes are relatively common in Drakma, especially in regions of the north and heartlands, areas known for their many mountains and highlands. Like most drakes they tend to prefer regions rich in minerals for them to consume, these are essential for their bones, muscles and mainly, their signature armored shell/scales.

Due to their size and armor, they are usually the apex predators of the regions they inhabit, few creatures can represent a threat to an adult Mountain Drake, and most are other species of Drake. Due to adults usually forming permanent pairs with their mates, it's hard to find one traveling alone.

Relationship with Humans

Since they live in areas that are usually difficult to traverse and where humans rarely live, accidental contact with adults tends to be quite rare. Most of the time Juveniles are the ones that traverse long distances. Most contacts are made by those hunting these drakes, their mineralized armor is very valuable, it can be melted to extract the ores imbued into them. Some from older specimens can be turned into drake steel, a highly valued material for smiths. But hunting these creatures is a dangerous task, and also requires permits insured by the local government or the Hunters Guild.
