Overview

Mountain Drakes are one of the most common, large drake species in Drakma. They can be found inhabiting much of the mountainous regions of the north and heartlands. These animals are especially known for their extremely resilient, mineral scale armor. They can often be found calmly sleeping in groups or roaming lonely across valleys between mountains. Normally they don't attack humans, not seeing them as real prey due to size.

Appearance

Mountain Drakes are large quadrupedal reptilians, like most drake species, they have a dense, mineralized, scale armor growing over their bodies, making them extremely resilient to damage. The color and appearance of the armor depends on the minerals consumed by the drake, the resistance of the armor is also influenced by this. Their scales are usually colored in green, reds, blues, grays and blacks. Their armors can make they look spiky, especially their tails, these also sometimes end up resembling large clubs.

Behavior

For the most part, Mountain Drakes don't attack small or medium-sized species, they prefer to hunt large prey, suc as the mountain bison, wyrms or even other drake species. Many small critters even began to associate them with safety, remaining close to these creatures for protection.

But this does not mean they are passive, if threatened of desperate they will viciously attack their target, this is especially devastating when they are in groups. These groups, of usually around 4 to 7 individuals are formed after mating season, they are temporary and tend to only last until they hatchlings grow old enough to accompany their mothers during travels.

Males tend to be larger and are especially aggressive if they are guarding eggs or young drakelings. In preparation for the mating season males make large nests using, branches, rocks and minerals. Usually the female that accepts them will eat the minerals, they help with the formations of the mineralized shells for their eggs.

Once the eggs hatch the young drakes will eat the shells to get the minerals from it, helping they develop their first mineralized scales. They remain with their parents for around a year and a half, after this period they are already old enough to care for themselves.

Habitat

Mountain Drakes tend to inhabit high elevation regions, like mountains, plateaus, highlands, etc. Juveniles tend to roam alone across these regions, while adults form pairs with their mates. After mating season multiple pairs might come together to create nests, this is mostly for protection of the eggs.

It's very rare to find adults living on low elevation areas, especially if they are warm. Due to their high natural body temperatures they prefer cooler climates. They also priorize regions with an abundance in minerals and ores, especially ferrous ones.

Abilities

  • Mineral Armor: Naturally their scales grow and harden into an armor as they age.
  • Magma Breath: The leftover slag from the minerals they eat can be expelled as magma balls.
  • Enhanced Muscles and Bones: Their muscles and bone are extremely dense, giving them super strength.
  • Temporary Hardening: They can temporarily harden body parts for defense or attack.

Ecology

Mountain Drakes are relatively common in Drakma, especially in regions of the north and heartlands, areas known for their many mountains and highlands. Like most drakes they tend to prefer regions rich in minerals for them to consume, these are essential for their bones, muscles and mainly, their signature armored shell/scales.

Due to their size and armor, they are usually the apex predators of the regions they inhabit, few creatures can represent a threat to an adult Mountain Drake, and most are other species of Drake. Due to adults usually forming permanent pairs with their mates, it's hard to find one traveling alone.

Relationship with Humans

Since they live in areas that are usually difficult to traverse and where humans rarely live, accidental contact with adults tends to be quite rare. Most of the time Juveniles are the ones that traverse long distances. Most contacts are made by those hunting these drakes, their mineralized armor is very valuable, it can be melted to extract the ores imbued into them. Some from older specimens can be turned into drake steel, a highly valued material for smiths. But hunting these creatures is a dangerous task, and also requires permits insured by the local government or the Hunters Guild.
