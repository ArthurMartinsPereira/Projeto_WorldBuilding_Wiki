Overview

The mighty Jungle Tyrants of Falkore are among the largest species of drake in Drakma and the tallest currently known. These enormous herbivores inhabit the nation's dense jungles, where they travel in family herds led by the oldest and most experienced members. Despite their peaceful nature, they can become extremely dangerous when threatened, particularly when protecting their young. A small number of Falkorean clans have developed secretive techniques for domesticating them.

Appearance

Jungle Tyrants are huge sauropod looking drakes, their necks and tail are long, with the tail being almost twice the length of the body. Like other drakes, they also grow a mineralized scale armor for protection, though theirs, due to the lack of minerals in their habitats, are far more sparce, only covering their shoulders, spine, legs and the base of the neck. The final section of the tail is covered in overlapping metallic scales that form a flexible cutting edge. The scales naturally wear down through use, forcing Jungle Tyrants to periodically sharpen them against exposed rock or the mineralized portions of their own bodies. Their scales come in various tones of green and brow, their tail blades and armored bits have a darker metalic color. Males have special fins and air-sacks on their necks covered in bright colors, they use this to attract mates.

Behavior

Jungle Tyrants tend to be relatively calm and social, often traveling in small or large family units, these are lead by the oldest and most experienced male or female of the herd. They prefer to nest in areas that are high but also close to sources of fresh water. Occasionally herds migrate to different regions, this mostly happens during mating season, they migrate to areas abundant in fruits and foliage they feed off.

These drakes are protective of their young and will attack any other creatures that might approach them if they sense danger. This has lead to most other animals, including predators, to avoid them, especially if they have drakelings with them. Their nests are made with large rocks, branches, logs and even mud, being built to last, many have even been used for generations by the same herds. Their nests are comunal, the whole hear maintains them and cares for the young.

Males tend to be larger and are colored in brighter colors, they possess special fins and air-sacks on their necks that they use for display during mating season. Females generally choose those with the brightest colors and impressive fins. Some males even try to damage the fins of their peers to sabotage their chances. Both males and females take good care of their tail blades, being their greatest weapon.

It's rare to find a solitary Jungle Tyrant, these are either young drakes that have left their original herds, or pariahs. The pariahs tend to be males whose fins have gotten too damaged or are too aggressive and were expelled, these can be extremely dangerous due to the stress and loneliness.

Habitat

Jungle Tyrants are native to the jungles and forests or Falkore, they can't be found anywhere else. The prefer regions rich in dense vegetation, especially fruit-bearing trees, and fresh water. In many of these areas its possible to find the paths created by the movement of these herds.

These animals are deeply respected by most local communities, mostly due to fear, so it's rare for humans to hunt them or expand into areas they inhabit. Though recently some groups have developed techniques to domesticate these giants, what has lead to humans to become more confident when contacting them.

Abilities

  • Tail Whip: Their long tails can be used as a whip, the blade at the end is extremely sharp.
  • Armor Enhancement: Can temporarily enhance their scale armor with geo or animus energy.
  • Elemental Shockwave: They can generate shock waves of elemental energy by stumping the ground.
  • Titanic Strength: They are one of the most physically strong species in the entire continent.

Ecology

Jungle Tyrants are one of the most well known species of Falkore, even being featured in the banner of the Forest Watch. They can be found in various regions of the nation, usually in herds or family unities. Most of the time they tend to remain calm and unbothered by humans and other small animals, but if they are protecting eggs or young drakes they can be very ferocious, lone pariahs also tend to be aggressive.

Many clearing and paths that cut through the jungle were formed by generations of Jungle Tyrant herds moving from region to region. For centuries these have been use as rudimentary roads for people or other animals to move more easily through the dense forests of Falkore. Jungle Tyrants have also shaped their homes in other ways, such as their large communal nests, they look like hollowed hills, covered in rocks, logs and branches.

Relationship with Humans

The relationship of humans with the Jungle Tyrants is one of both respect and fear, for generations these animals have been revered as simples of power and strength, seem as the undisputed kings of the jungle. Attacks on human settlements and caravans are rare, but they have happened enough time to warrant caution when near these animals. Over centuries some clans developed ways of communicating and domesticating these creatures, now days they can be found helping to transport people or heavy cargos across the forests.
