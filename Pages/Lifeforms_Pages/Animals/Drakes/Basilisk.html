Basilisk

A dangerous species of Drake that inhabits cavernous and mountainous regions.

Overview

Basilisks are a species belonging to the Drake family, they process plates of mineralized scales over their bodies for protection, but their most well known feature is their ability to "petrify" other creatures. Their special venom can force the muscles of their victims to become completely rigid, making them look like statues.

Appearance

Basilisks have reptilian bodies, like most drakes, their bodies are covered a layer of hard, mineralized scales that protect them from most points, except their belies. Their limbs are thick and muscular, allowing them to easily crush bones and stone. Their tails are quite short compared to other drakes. They come in shades of gray, green, browns, blues and reds, it depends on the location where they live and the minerals available there.

Behavior

Basilisks are solitary and territorial creatures, most of the time they prefer to live in isolated locations, such as mountains or surface caves. Their metabolisms are slow, allowing them to remain long periods in hibernation, during this time they cur up and stay very still, it's easy to confuse them for rocks, and many travelers have accidentally entered their nests.

Throughout most of the year Basilisks remain solitary, only during the mating season is when they search for partners, especially the females, they are larger and usually search for multiple males. After mating they return to their nests and lay a clutch of 3 to 5 eggs, the mother cares for them until they hatch, after that the young remain close to her until around 1 year, after that they leave.

Basilisks have a habit of stocking food by over saturating their prey with their venom, the bodies become so hardened that they even roting is slowed down. These victims usually end up dying but since the saturation is slow they can be saved if they receive the antivenom also produced by the Basilisk. Basilisks use the antivenom to make it easier to eat the prey, after being "petrified" the target remains is a kind of suspended animation. The process of saturation also makes the corpses uneatable for other creatures, both due to the hardness and the venom.

Habitat

Basilisks like cold and dark places, so they usually make their lairs in caves and mountains. Thanks to their slow metabolism they only have to eat a few times a year, this makes it hard for prey animals to adapt to their presence, since they are not constantly active.

Due to choosing fairly isolated locations, contact with humans is quite rare, most of the time they just attack livestock or wild animals. Occasionally poachers have tried to slay or capture Basilisks, but usually when they aren't stopped by the Hunters Guild, the basilisks are able to hunt them instead.

Abilities

  • Petrifying Venom
  • Powerful Claws and Jaw
  • Stone Scales
  • Anti Venom

Ecology

Basilisks are a relatively rare species of drake, due to their habitats and competition among their own kind, their numbers remain fairly low. Their impact on the regions they inhabit is manageable, the only times they cause problems is if a mother is defending her nest or when they target livestock. Though there have been a few cases of lost travelers being attacked.

Relationship with Humans

Most of the time Basilisks and Humans barely interact, due to the isolated locations they inhabit Basilisks aren't frequently hunted, in fact the only thing in them that is even worth the hunt is their venom and antivenom. Their scales, although resilient,aren't on the level of other large drake species, making them almost worthless to merchants. Their venom on the other hand, is highly valuable, especially in the black market and criminal underworld.
