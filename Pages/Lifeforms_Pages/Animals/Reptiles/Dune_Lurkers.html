Dune Lurker

A large species of snake that inhabits the various deserts of Dragonfell.

Overview

A rare and huge species of snake that can be found in certain regions of Dragonfell, known for burying their bodies under the sands and sand to ambush prey. They can constrict around their victims and harden their scales with Geo energy to crush more easily, they are also known for having a special toxin that can put targets to sleep or in a trance like state.

Appearance

Dune Lurkers have long serpentine bodies, they possess special scales on their sides that are naturally pointier and harder than normal, these can be further enhanced with geo energy.

they can come in a variety of colors to help then camouflage to the environments they live in, from browns, to oranges and even the golden tones of the desert dunes.

At the tip of their tails they grow a large club like appendage, similar to the rattle of a rattlesnake, though completely solid, and very hard. They often use this natural club as a weapon to subdue their prey.

They also possess two small "horns" on their snouts, these are in fact, special sensory organs they use to detect prey when they are buried in sand.

Behavior

Dune Lurkers are one of the largest predator species found in the deserts of Dragonfell, mainly inhabiting the border regions of the nation, where the climate is less extreme.

Much of the time they will simply lay hidden and wait, burying themselves in the sand and remaining still for long periods of time. Their bodies can endure the heat of the desert pretty well, they have two horn looking sensory organs on their snouts that they use to sense vibrations and detect prey.

Once the prey is close enough the Sand Viper will lunge in a surprise attack, either trying to bite into the target or to strike them with their huge tail club. If they can get close enough they will try to imobilize their prey with a crushing constricting hug.

Once the prey is dead or unconscious the serpent will quickly begin to swallow them whole. Depending on the size of their prey they might spend weeks without needing to eat, though they also become more vulnerable in this state.

During mating season, the females start to produce pheromones that leave trails for the males to follow, the female will look for a safe place such as a cave or burrow, once a male finds her they mate.

After mating the male leaves and after some time the female will lay a clutch of eggs, she will care for them for some time, but eventually will also leave. After hatching the young serpents will scatter across the surrounding area and try to survive.

As hatchlings, they are pretty vulnerable, but the hardship they have at this young age is what allows for them to eventually become the apex predators they are known as.

Habitat

For the most part the Dune Lurkers inhabit the bordering regions of Dragonfell, where the climate is still harsh and arid but also tolerable enough for them to survive.
