Monstrosities - Drakma

Catalog of the monstrosity category of monsters

Chimera

A kind of artificial monster borne from the combination of traits from multiple other creatures. Found in most of Drkama, these creatures come in various shapes and size, varying according to species

Curse Beasts

A kind of artificial monster created to carry and spread curses, overt time they mutated, becoming infectious and birthing the lycanthropic curse and many other kinds of werecreatures.
