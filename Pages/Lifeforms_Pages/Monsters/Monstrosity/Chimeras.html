Overview

Appearance

The appearances of Curse Beasts varies a lot depending on the type and subspecies. Lican ones have a more canine or wolf like look, others may look more human-like such as the wendigos, there are also some that look like vultures, boars, bears, and many other animals. One thing that must be noted is that despite having various similar traits, even individuals from the same subspecies might differ in appearance somewhat, looking more or less monstrous or mutated.

Behavior

Almost all Curse Beasts are completely savage and rabid in nature, they will attack anything that is not one of their own, be it human, monster or animal. More often than not their victims are either killed, die from their injuries or the curse implanted into them, though in the rare case a creature or person survives, there is the chance that they have become hosts of the curse.

Curse Beasts only live to spread the curse around, since that is how they reproduce after all. Aside from the wendigos they don't seem to infect others on purpose, it simply happens during their attacks, generally these monsters don't have much intelligence, but older ones can be quite cunning in their approach when hunting.

Curse Beasts usually only live up to 10 years ofter turning, as time passes the curse within them takes a toll on their bodies and starts to eat away at their soul. Eventually they are completely hollowed out and just become a husk. Though it has been observed that some rare individuals can adapt and live much longer.

Habitat

Curse beasts can inhabit all environments, each subspecies carrying adaptations that help them survive and adapt. Some live in deep forests and jungles, others in deserts or swamps, even the skies were claimed by some, while the sea seems to be safe for now.

In Drakma, curse beasts are more commonly found in the central and western part of the continent, but they have also reached Falkore and Northwind in the last century. The Hunters Guild has been working hard to exterminate these creatures for a long time, but until now their efforts at best only slowed down the spread and made attacks on humans rarer.

Abilities

  • Curse Strike
  • Powerful Musculature
  • Adaptability
  • Immunity to Lesser Curses

Ecology

Curse Beasts are considered a plague anywhere they are found, they don't belong in nature and always end up creating an imbalance in any ecosystem they come into contact with. This is mostly due to their unrestrained aggression, and the drive to spread their curse, many times they kill other creatures for no reason, simply leaving the corpses behind. Even some species of magical creatures have begun to develop ways of dealing with Curse Beasts, these vary from ambushes, ways of hiding their magic signature, or for some, simply acting just as aggressive towards them.

Relationship with Humans

Since they were created by humans, Curse Beasts have a long story with them, and for all of it there had always been hostility between them. Curse beasts were originally created to serve as weapons of war, so from their very conception all they knew was rage and pain. This caused them to develop a kind of vendetta against humanity, they will always prioritize killing or infecting the humans they come into contact with. Humans in turn have spent the last few hundred years trying to exterminate Curse Beasts and correct their mistake.
