Overview

Chimeras are one of the many monsters created to serve as weapons of war, in their case they are less of a species and more like a whole category of monsters that are classified as chimeric aberrations. In general any creatures that were creature through the combination of traits from multiple others are referred to as chimeras. Most of the time they can't reproduce normally, but given enough time, go through a kind of mitosis. Chimeras can be quite hard to kill, especially due to their fast regeneration. Due to inheriting the instincts of the animals that compose them they usually find a niche in the environments they inhabit, and although not perfect, they cause far less damage than other monstrosities.

Appearance

The appearances of Chimeras varies a lot depending on the animals they are made of. One thing that is noticeable is that the traits they carry are from animals that would be of a similar or smaller size to the chimera itself. There are exceptions to this but due to the instability that incompatible traits carry, it's not common.

Chimeras can be covered in scales, fur, feathers, rubbery or smooth skin, bone plantings and more, they can have more or less limbs or appendages, even multiple heads. The rarest examples can even carry traits from magic creatures.

One rare phenomenon that has been observed is that some elder individuals may develop the capacity to absorb traits from their prey and even pass these down through mitosis to the next generation of chimeras.

Behavior

Chimeras tend to inherit most of their behavioral traits from the same creatures used to form their bodies. Due to this they do tend to behave more like animals, unlike many other kinds of monsters, such as the Curse Beasts. But do to their traits coming from different species, they can sometimes get in conflict with one another.

Ecology

Curse Beasts are considered a plague anywhere they are found, they don't belong in nature and always end up creating an imbalance in any ecosystem they come into contact with. This is mostly due to their unrestrained aggression, and the drive to spread their curse, many times they kill other creatures for no reason, simply leaving the corpses behind. Even some species of magical creatures have begun to develop ways of dealing with Curse Beasts, these vary from ambushes, ways of hiding their magic signature, or for some, simply acting just as aggressive towards them.

Relationship with Humans

Since they were created by humans, Curse Beasts have a long story with them, and for all of it there had always been hostility between them. Curse beasts were originally created to serve as weapons of war, so from their very conception all they knew was rage and pain. This caused them to develop a kind of vendetta against humanity, they will always prioritize killing or infecting the humans they come into contact with. Humans in turn have spent the last few hundred years trying to exterminate Curse Beasts and correct their mistake.
