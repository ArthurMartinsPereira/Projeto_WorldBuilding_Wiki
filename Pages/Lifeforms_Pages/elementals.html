The Elemental Lifeforms of Drakma

Catalog of the known elemental lifeforms found across Drakma

Slimes

One of the most common kinds of elemental life forms, they aren't particularly powerful or dangerous on their own, usualy they can be found in areas with dence elemental energy or where the Lay Lines have started to Overflow. Slimes usualy are formed of one of the 7 Natural/Lesser elements, over time they may evolve in more complex forms.

Constructs

A slightly more advanced elemental life form than the slimes, Constructs usualy have hard shells over their bodies and come in a variety of geometric shappes. As they age they may grow in size and gain a more coherent shape, like slimes they tend to be formed of one of the 7 Lesser elements, though rarely ones made of the Light or Darkness elements can also be found.

Wisps

Also known as Will-O-Wisps, these are weak, mostly gazous kind of elemental lifeform, they can be found in swamps and other humid or foggy areas. Wisps come in various collors, these are usualy related to the element they are made off, they also emit a weak glow in the dark. Their cores and some small shards are the only phisical parts of a Wisp.

Eidolon

Eidolons are the apex of what all elemental lifeforms try to become, being the oldest and most powerful of their kind, their control over elemental energy is so refined they may even generate unique variants of these elements. Eidolons are not only rare but also extremely dangerous, they come in various forms, often resembling mythical or extinct creatures.
