Factions of Drakma

Catalog of the most relevant organizations in Drakma.

The Hunters Guild

An organization dedicated to hunting down or containing dangerous creatures, fighting magical threats and protecting the balance of the natural world.

Heavenrock Magic Academy

A famous school of magic that has existed for thousands of years. Being extremely influential in many nations and the birthplace of Path Magic.

Crimson Brotherhood

A dangerous and secretive organization, little is known about its members and objectives, only that they wish to help humanity ascend, they often abduct people for experiments.

Pelagia Research Institute

A renowned organization of Pelagia, specialized in the research and development of new magical technologies, such as the Elemental Automatons.
