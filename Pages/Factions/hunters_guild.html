Overview

The Hunters Guild is an organization dedicated to fight against magical threats and protect the balance and stability of the natural world and humanity. They especially hunt down monsters or dangerous species that are causing trouble for civilians or ecosystems.

The Guild was founded roughly over 900 years ago, since then it has grown and changed a lot, its operations extent across all of Drakma, having different Branches in each of the nations.

The main Headquarters of the Guild are located in Wyrmcrest, the nation of origin of the Guild. Each branch of the Guild has its own regional base of operations and generally have a lot of freedom on how to manage themselves. This is due to these branches being formed by different groups that already operated in these nations and were simply absorbed by or united with the Guild.

The Guild has many different groups working inside it to keep it operating, but for the most part, the largest ones can be classified in three main divisions, the Hunters Division, the Medical Division and the Engineering Division.

Membership

There are a few different ways of joining the Guild depending on which branch is the one recruiting. But for the most part, any aspiring member must go through an entrance exam. This exam will test different skills depending on the division they are trying to join and the age of the participant.

The most well known of these exams is the one for the Hunters Division that happens every 2 or 3 years. In this one the participants are taken to a location under the Guild's jurisdiction, there they will have to hunt, capture or gather information on the creatures and report back until evening. The participants are constantly monitored and if in danger a hunter may intervene, but the participant will be disqualified. Those who complete the task will be accepted as Recruits.

Those who pass the exams will be allowed to join the Guild, at first they will be under watch to make sure they adapt or that they aren't causing problems. If all goes well they become permanent members, if they don't meet the expectatives they are expelled. On the case of the Hunters Division, they are divided into parties, with one more experienced instructor, 2 recruits of the hunters division and 1 of the medical one.

In the Hunters Division the members are divided in a kind of hierarchy, it separates the more experienced and strong members from newer ones. Those higher on the hierarchy receive more dangerous or important missions.
