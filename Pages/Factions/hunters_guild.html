Overview

The Hunters Guild is an organization dedicated to fighting magical threats and protecting the balance and stability of the natural world and human civilization. Its members are especially concerned with hunting, containing, studying, or relocating monsters and other dangerous species that threaten civilians, settlements, or local ecosystems.

The Guild was founded roughly over 900 years ago, since then it has grown and changed a lot, its operations extent across all of Drakma, having different Branches in each of the nations.

The main Headquarters of the Guild are located in Wyrmcrest, the nation of origin of the Guild. Each branch of the Guild has its own regional base of operations and generally have a lot of freedom on how to manage themselves. This is due to these branches being formed by different groups that already operated in these nations and were simply absorbed by or united with the Guild.

The Guild has many different groups working inside it to keep it operating, but for the most part, the largest ones can be classified in three main divisions, the Hunters Division, the Medical Division and the Research Division.

The Three Divisions

Hunters Division

This is the oldest and most important among the divisions, being responsible for most of the heavy work done by the Guild. Various groups are part of the Hunters Division, these include: Hunters, Trackers, Trappers, Outriders, among others.

This division is commanded mostly by the Captains of each branch, they are responsible for reporting to HQ, dividing tasks and taking on the most important missions. The Hunters Division requires the hunters to be divided in parties of 4, 3 are hunters with one being the leader, 1 is a field medic of the Medical Division, only master hunters are allowed to work alone.

Medical Division

The Medical Division is responsible for giving support for the hunters, help injured civilians and take care of evacuations, safe zones and medical centers.

The members of this division include field medics, nurses, surgeons, pharmacists, doctors, etc. The field medics in particular are the ones that accompany hunters during missions, for this they also receive basic combat training.

Research Division

The Research Division is responsible for many aspects of the Guild like, research on animals and monsters, archeology, manufacturing of equipment, management of supplies, infrastructure and more.

This is the division with the most variety in its workers, from smiths, artisans and inventors to historians, archeologists and biologists. New members may start as simple assistants and eventually be come independent researchers.

Most of the time they do not work on field with hunters, preferring to remain in safe installations of the Guild, unless they are transporting important things or for specific studies on dangerous subjects and locations.

History

The Hunters Guild was originally founded around 900 years ago, in Wyrmcrest, at the time, although there were individuals that performed the role of Hunters, there wasn't a unified organization to support them.

In some locations hunters were even feared or hated due to misconceptions and rumors, mostly spread by the Magic Academy mages, so Hunters had little support from society.

But after a few events, some members of the royal were saved by a passing hunter, this is what started to increase their reputation. Eventually he created the beginnings of what would one day become the Hunters Guild, they were given an old abandoned fortress, that was eventually turned into their HQ.

Membership

There are a few different ways of joining the Guild depending on which branch is the one recruiting. But for the most part, any aspiring member must go through an entrance exam. This exam will test different skills depending on the division they are trying to join and the age of the participant.

The most well known of these exams is the one for the Hunters Division that happens every 2 or 3 years. In this one the participants are taken to a location under the Guild's jurisdiction, there they will have to hunt, capture or gather information on the creatures and report back until evening. The participants are constantly monitored and if in danger a hunter may intervene, but the participant will be disqualified. Those who complete the task will be accepted as Recruits.

Those who pass the exams will be allowed to join the Guild, at first they will be under watch to make sure they adapt or that they aren't causing problems. If all goes well they become permanent members, if they don't meet the expectatives they are expelled. On the case of the Hunters Division, they are divided into parties, with one more experienced instructor, 2 recruits of the hunters division and 1 of the medical one.

In the Hunters Division the members are divided in a kind of hierarchy, it separates the more experienced and strong members from newer ones. Those higher on the hierarchy receive more dangerous or important missions.

Hierarchy

The hierarchy of power in the Hunters Guild works mostly according to its divisions, each have their own leaders that have to report back to the Guild Master and HQ. In the case of the Hunters Division the ones in charge are the Captains.

The Guild Master is the main one responsible for the Guild's affairs and decisions, they also receive support from a council senior members of various divisions. The Guild Master does not need to be from the Hunters Division or even a powerful fighter, only a capable leader.

The Hunters Division's hierarchy is usually considered, more complex than the other two. When new members first join they start as Recruits, them they move on to Apprentice, Novice, Hunter, Veteran, Elite, Master and them they may be chosen for Captain or Vice-Captain. Only those on Master rank can become Vice-Captains or Captains.

There is no age restriction for one to increase their rank, as long as one proves themselves capable they can raise. Of course seniority and experience can help a lot depending on the division. Sometimes to rank up a member might have to pass a test, it depends on the division, though if the member has already proven themselves they can rank up without it too.

Leadership and Management

The main leader of the Guild is the Guild Master, usually a senior member chosen from one of the three main divisions. They are aided by a council of other senior members and are responsible from managing guild affairs, like trading, approving budgets and certain missions and more.

Each branch of the Guild in other nations have their own versions of the three divisions, and these are managed by their own leaders, like the Hunters Division, each branch has its own Captain.

Foreign Relations

The Guild has, in general, good relations with all nations and independent regions of Drakma. In some they even have full support of the local government to operate, like in Wyrmcrest.

Each nation has its own branch of the Guild, these were usually formed by groups that already operated as hunters in those regions and simply merged with the Guild later. For this reason most of these branches are allowed to have their own rules and codes of conduct up to a certain extent.

Although the Guild has tried its best to keep good relations with the governments where it operates, they have faced some challenges in certain regions, like Heavenrock, Pelagia and the Wildlands.
