Overview

The Crimson Brotherhood is an infamous organization that has been causing many problems across the continent for decades. Little is known about them and their objectives, they often kidnap people for experiments, for what purpose they do such things is still a mystery.

Many reports have registered sightings of their members operating in various countries across Drakma, in many cases, after these sightings magical disaster start to occur, disappearances of people and monsters also are common.

The exact time of their founding is still a mystery, but its known that they have been operating in the shadows for decades, but in recent times they have become far more aggressive in their pursues.

It's extremely difficult to extract any useful information from captured members, most of the time they kill themselves or become catatonic. Though a few key findings have been acquired, the Brotherhood seems to be trying to "advance" or "evolve" humanity somehow.

How the Brotherhood Operates

Scouts, Spies and Infiltrators

Most of the members of the Brotherhood prefer to work in the shadows, gathering information, kidnapping useful people, performing assassinations, etc.

For decades the Brotherhood was able to remain completely hidden from most countries and organizations, only after they started to grow bolder with their actions, they became a known as a threat.

Incursions

Rarely the Brotherhood has performed large scale attacks against various institutions and locations. Usually aiming at stealing information, resources or any other thing that might interest them.
