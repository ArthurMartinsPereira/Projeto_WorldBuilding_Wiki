The Crimson Brotherhood

A mysterious and infamous organization, considered extremely dangerous by many.

Overview

The Crimson Brotherhood is an infamous organization that has caused numerous problems across the continent for decades. Little is known about the organization or its objectives. Its members are known to kidnap people for unknown experiments, though the exact purpose of these experiments remains a mystery.

Reports of Brotherhood members operating in various countries across Drakma have been recorded for decades. In many cases, their appearances have been followed by magical disasters, disappearances and the emergence of strange creatures.

The exact date of the Brotherhood's founding remains unknown. It is known, however, that the organization has operated in the shadows for decades. In recent years, the Brotherhood has become increasingly aggressive, carrying out larger and more frequent operations.

Extracting useful information from captured members is extremely difficult. Most either kill themselves or become catatonic during interrogation. Nevertheless, a few important findings have been obtained.

The Brotherhood appears to be pursuing some form of advancement or evolution of humanity. The exact meaning of this objective, however, remains unknown.

How the Brotherhood Operates

Scouts, Spies and Infiltrators

Most members of the Brotherhood prefer to operate from the shadows. Their activities include gathering information, kidnapping individuals of interest, performing assassinations and infiltrating organizations.

For decades, the Brotherhood was able to remain almost completely hidden from most nations and organizations. Only after its members began acting more boldly did the organization become widely recognized as a significant threat.

Incursions

On rare occasions, the Brotherhood has carried out large-scale attacks against institutions and other important locations. These operations generally aim to steal information, resources, magical artifacts or any other object that may be of interest to the organization.

The scale of these attacks has increased over the years. Several nations have officially declared the Crimson Brotherhood a terrorist organization, though such declarations appear to have little effect on its activities.

Experiments and Creations

The Brotherhood is primarily known for its strange, cruel and inhuman experiments and creations. Many of these creations are used during attacks, while others are released into the wild when considered failures.

These creations include mutated humans and animals, artificial monsters, unstable magical entities, dangerous mechanisms and many other experimental creations.

The Brotherhood has also successfully developed several unique technologies and magical devices, many of which are used to strengthen its members or enhance its experiments.

The organization frequently searches for forbidden magical knowledge and is willing to go to great lengths to acquire it. It also appears to welcome individuals rejected or persecuted by mainstream society.

History

Little is known about the origins of the Brotherhood. No one seems to know who founded the organization or what its original purpose was.

Captured members generally know only the most basic information about the organization and appear to follow orders from their superiors without understanding the larger picture.

One piece of information has appeared repeatedly during interrogations: the Brotherhood seems to seek the advancement of humanity and the achievement of some greater form of existence.

What this advancement means remains unknown. Different members have provided different interpretations, and it is possible that even the Brotherhood itself is divided regarding the organization's ultimate objective.

Membership

How members are selected and accepted into the Brotherhood is mostly unknown. For the most part, individuals appear to be chosen according to their potential and capabilities, although lower-ranking members are often ordinary people recruited from the fringes of society.

The Brotherhood appears to particularly target individuals who are unhappy or suffering because of the current state of society. Such people are promised a better life and the opportunity to help change the world.

Many members also appear to possess personal vendettas against specific nations, organizations or social groups. Some are feared even by their fellow members because of their behavior or personalities.

The Brotherhood clearly possesses some form of leadership. However, the identity of its highest-ranking leader, or leaders, remains a mystery.

High-ranking members are known to receive orders from a figure or group above them, but the exact nature of this leadership remains unknown to most members.

Hierarchy

Based on the information gathered from captured members, the Brotherhood appears to possess a relatively simple hierarchical structure.

Most members are ordinary people who serve as informants, spies or scouts. Above them are operatives responsible for more dangerous activities such as kidnapping, murder, theft and sabotage.

Above these operatives are commanders who manage lower-ranking members and organize larger operations. These commanders are often responsible for leading incursions and major missions.

Above them is an unknown higher authority that appears to control the organization as a whole.

Leadership and Management

From the perspective of most members, the commanders represent the highest level of authority within the Brotherhood. They issue orders, manage operations and lead important missions.

However, evidence suggests that even the commanders must report to a higher authority. The identity of this authority, and whether it consists of a single individual or a larger group, remains completely unknown.

Foreign Relations

As expected, the Crimson Brotherhood has extremely poor relations with almost every nation and major organization in Drakma.

The few groups willing to do business with the Brotherhood are generally criminal organizations or other extremist groups. Even these groups are known to be extremely cautious when dealing with the Brotherhood.

In recent years, the Hunters Guild has frequently clashed with the Brotherhood. The Guild has managed to stop several incursions, prevent multiple kidnappings and destroy numerous experimental creations released by the organization.
