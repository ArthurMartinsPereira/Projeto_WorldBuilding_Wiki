Notable Locations

Headquarters and Branches

  • Dorsek City — The second capital of Northwind, build after the fall of the first capital, Windrise, to the dragon Vaelkros. Located in a magically warm zone near the frozen lands to the north.
  • Helstorm — the largest city of the Great Tundra, it serves as the local trading center, being one of the most important settlements of the eastern side of Northwind, only behind the capital.
  • Valrokk — A city located on the shores of a large lake in the Shrouded Taiga, the main route to the west of Northwind crosses through here.
  • Yorik's Shield — A fortress city build close to the Howling Canion, it's mostly used for defencive purposes and to regulate entrance into the kingdom through the canion.
