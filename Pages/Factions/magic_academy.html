The Magic Academy of Heavenrock

The oldest institution dedicate to the research and teaching of magic arts in Drakma.

Overview

The Magic Academy is probably the oldest organization in Drakma, dating back to the early days of the Age of Heroes, it was founded by Nox, the first Archmage and father of Path Magic.

For thousands of years the Academy has continued to research and refine magic, and over time it has changed almost completely from what it used to be in its early days. Now days it has become far more elitist than in its origin, only accepting those with talent, influence or money to join the Academy.

A few centuries ago, the Magic Academy was considered the most powerful and influential organization in the continent, though after the formation of the Hunters Guild and its spread across Drakma, much of that influence has evaporated.

This lead many of the more conservative members of the Magic Academy to develop a one-sided rivalry with the Guild, often trying to prove their superiority, though there are still reasonable people in the academy.

Inner Workings of the Academy

Education

The Academy mainly known for teaching one thing, Path Magic, they take students from all across the continent, but only the most talented or those belonging to important families are accepted.

Classes range from spell casting and development, alchemy, enchanting, magical item creation and even politics. There are different disciplines when it comes to Path Magic, raging from combat, healing, crafting, defense, support, among others.

Research and Development

The academy has made its duty to continuously research and advance Path Magic since its conception by Nox, over time it has evolved to become one of humanities most complex and varied fields of study.

To this day one of the main requirements to becoming and Archmage is to have created a truly revolutionary spell, or to have helped to push Path Magic further in some other innovative ways.

Politics

Originally the Academy and its mages were not really into politics, their founder, Nox, had little interest in such things. But over the centuries, as the Academy's reputation in Heavenrock grew, the nobles approached them offering funding and support.

Over time many nobbles joined the Academy, at the same time they became more reliant on their funding. Many mages started to serve as counselors for important people, even the royal family.

After thousands of years after its founding, little is left of the original Academy, now days one of its major focuses is increasing its political influence. As an organization it has also become far more elitist over time.

History

The Magic Academy was originally founded during the Age of Heroes by Nox, the first Archmage, a legendary figure and the father of Path Magic. At the time, the organization was nothing more than a small school dedicated to teaching Nox's discoveries and pushing Path magic further.

Though eventually, after many decades, one day Nox simply disappeared, for years he had slowly been becoming more cryptic and isolated. Most of the scrolls and manuscripts he left behind were writen in a mysterious codified language, to this day much of it hasn't been translated.

After Nox's disappearance the academy entered a period of decline for many centuries, although his students tried their best to keep going. Eventually the Academy was able to rise again, this time greater than ever before and its influence spread across many nations of Drakma.

Membership

To join the Magic Academy now days the two most important pre-requisites are, coming from a wealthy or very influential family or being talented enough to get the attention of the organization.

Unless deemed necessary the students don't need to go through an entrance exam or other tests. Depending on wht they wish to study they might not even need to be capable of using magic.

Once one joins the Academy they start as simple students, but if they prove their worth they might gain certain benefits and duties.

The average time for a student to graduate is between 4 and 7 years, it varies according to their area(s) of study and how quickly they progress. By the end they may be officially declared Mages, Enchanters, Sorcerers, Alchemists, Artificers, etc. It depends on what they graduated for.

Hierarchy

The academy has a varied hierarchical structure, going from junior students to the Archmages, students for the most part are all on equal footing, though usually older ones are still treated with respect by their peers.

Graduated mages and teachers are also an important part of the academy, being the ones responsible for selecting and teaching new students. At the top of the hierarchy are the Conclave of Mages and the two principals of the Academy.

Leadership and Management

For the most part the true leaders of the Magic Academy are the members of the Conclave of Mages and the two Principals that manage the academy.

Aside from the magic academy, the Conclave is also in charge of managing mages in general, at least those who have studied at the Academy.

Foreign Relations

In the past the Magic Academy had a pretty strong presence in over half on the continent, but over time they declined a bit. Though now days they are still very influential in nations like Heavenrock, Wyrmcrest, Varlon and Northwind.

While the original and flagship campus of the Academy is still located in Heavenrock, they have two other branches, one located in Wyrmcrest and another in Northwind. They had a few more in the past, but they have been closed down.

Heavenrock is the nation where they possess the most influence and power, especially due to the Court Mages that serve under noble houses as trusted advisors. Many outsiders even believe the academy are the real rulers of the nation.
