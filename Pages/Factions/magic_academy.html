Overview

The Magic Academy is probably the oldest organization in Drakma, dating back to the early days of the Age of Heroes, it was founded by Nox, the first Archmage and father of Path Magic.

For thousands of years the Academy has continued to research and refine magic, and over time it has changed almost completely from what it used to be in its early days. Now days it has become far more elitist than in its origin, only accepting those with talent, influence or money to join the Academy.

A few centuries ago, the Magic Academy was considered the most powerful and influential organization in the continent, though after the formation of the Hunters Guild and its spread across Drakma, much of that influence has evaporated.

This lead many of the more conservative members of the Magic Academy to develop a one-sided rivalry with the Guild, often trying to prove their superiority, though there are still reasonable people in the academy.

Inner Workings of the Academy

Education

The Academy mainly known for teaching one thing, Path Magic, they take students from all across the continent, but only the most talented or those belonging to important families are accepted.

Classes range from spell casting and development, alchemy, enchanting, magical item creation and even politics. There are different disciplines when it comes to Path Magic, raging from combat, healing, crafting, defense, support, among others.

Research and Development

The academy has made its duty to continuously research and advance Path Magic since its conception by Nox, over time it has evolved to become one of humanities most complex and varied fields of study.

To this day one of the main requirements to becoming and Archmage is ot have created a truly revolutionary spell, or to have helped to push Path Magic further in some other way.
