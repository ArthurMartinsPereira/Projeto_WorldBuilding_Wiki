The Pelagia Research Institute

An organization dedicated to the study, development and deployment of magic technology.

Overview

The Pelagia Research Institute is a relatively new organization, having been founded around a century ago. It aims at developing new technologies, often combined with magic to improve the development of the nation.

It has become one of the most important an influential factions within Pelagia, it was here that the Elemental Automatons used across the nation were first developed.

The institute was originally founded by ____ ____, a renowned inventor of his time, with the support of the royal family. Since its creation the organization has been constantly helping expand the field of magical technologies.

A few decades ago many noble families of Pelagia began to heavily invest into the Institute, this lead to them gain a lot more control over the organization. Over the years many researchers ended up leaving the organization due to the constant interference and pressure from these nobles.

The Inner Workings of the Institute

Engineers, Inventors, Scientists and Artificers

The Pelagia Research Institute is home to people from a wide variety of fields. Engineers, inventors, scientists and artificers all work within the organization, often cooperating on the same projects.

Engineers are usually responsible for designing and improving the mechanical and structural aspects of new technologies. Scientists study the properties of magic, elemental energy and other phenomena, while artificers specialize in applying magic to objects and machines. Inventors, meanwhile, often work across multiple fields, combining existing knowledge to create entirely new devices.

The boundaries between these professions are not always clear. A single researcher may be both an engineer and an artificer, or a scientist and an inventor. In practice, the Institute values results and expertise more than titles.

Most major projects require the cooperation of several departments. The creation of an Elemental Automaton, for example, may require researchers capable of studying elemental energy, artificers capable of binding it to machinery, and engineers capable of designing the machine itself.

The Grand Archive

One of the most important sectors of the Institute, here is where diagrams, reports, research papers, prototipes and more are stored. Usually access to these is restricted to only members of the organization, though occasionally outsiders might receive permission.

The Grand Archive are one of the largest reservoir of knowledge in Drakma, though its focus is on storing things related to technology.

Research Bureau

The Research Bureau is responsible for organizing and overseeing most of the Institute's research. It evaluates proposals, distributes funding and resources, assigns researchers to projects and reviews the results of completed experiments.

Research projects are usually proposed by individual researchers or teams. Before a project can begin, it must be evaluated by the Bureau, which determines its potential benefits, costs and risks. Projects involving dangerous magic, powerful elemental phenomena or experimental technologies may require additional approval.

The Bureau also decides which discoveries are made available to the public, which are sold to the government or private organizations, and which are kept confidential. This has become increasingly controversial as noble families have gained more influence over the Institute. They frequently use this influence to try and manipulate decisions in their favor.

History

The Research Institute was originally founded by a recluse but visionary inventor, ____ ____, he laid the ground for most of the fields of study that the organization still researches and improves to this day.

At the time of its creation the Institute was a very small organization, ____ provided all the funds himself and was very selective with who he allowed to join his organization.

Throughout his life the Institute experienced periods of growth and decline. Its founder often refused outside funding, fearing that wealthy patrons would attempt to control his work.

This changed when he was approached by King Edmund, the ruler of Pelagia at the time. The King offered the Institute a large amount of funding, resources and access to royal facilities in exchange for its assistance in improving the nation.

The founder initially refused, fearing that accepting royal support would compromise the Institute's independence. However, after negotiations, an agreement was reached that allowed the Institute to retain control over its research while cooperating with the royal government on projects considered important to the nation.

The agreement transformed the Institute. With access to resources that its founder could never have provided alone, it expanded rapidly and began attracting researchers from across Pelagia.

Membership

Now days is relatively easy for one to join the Institute, the two main ways are, already being well known and renowned in your field of study, or passing an entrance exam.

Those who go through the exam start up as assistants to more experienced members, over time as they gain their own experience they may "graduate" and receive higher positions.

As long as someone can prove they are capable there is a place for them in the organization. Although, due to the greater influence the nobles have acquired in recent years, they have been putting their own people to work inside the Institute.

This has lead to a rift forming within the organization, on one side there are the researchers that have gotten there with their own effort, and on the other those who work for and serve the nobles.

Hierarchy

The hierarchy of the Institute is relatively simple, the lowest members are the trainees and assistants, then you have the independent graduated researchers, them there are the senior researchers, and them the Head Researchers are at the top.

There are other people working at the Institute too, like janitors, cooks, accountants, etc.

Leadership and Management

In theory the senior and Head Researchers are the ones with the highest authority on the organization, they make most of the important decisions and take charge of various projects.

But in recent years, with the nobles gaining more and more influence, many of the decisions the institute takes have to also go through them for approval.

Foreign Relations

The Institute only has connections with a few nations of Drakma, particularly Kelrian, Garmorys and Wyrmcrest, though recently they have been trying to form connections in Heavenrock and Northwind.

Due to how young and small the institute still is when compared to organizations such as the Hunters Guild and Magic Academy, many used to underestimate it and it's capabilities.

But for the past twenty years their reputation has been steadily increasing, mostly thanks to some of their inventions, like the airships, elemental automatons, elemental suppressors and more.
