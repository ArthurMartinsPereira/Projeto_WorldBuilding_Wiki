Overview

The Pelagia Research Institute is a relatively new organization, having been founded around a century ago. It aims at developing new technologies, often combined with magic to improve the development of the nation.

It has become one of the most important an influential factions within Pelagia, it was here that the Elemental Automatons used across the nation were first developed.
