The Lay Lines

The invisible veins of magical energy that flow throughout the world.

What Are the Lay Lines?

The Lay Lines are vast currents of magical energy that flow throughout the world, functioning similarly to a circulatory system for magic itself. They contain, transport, and distribute magical energy across the land, helping maintain the balance of elemental and spiritual forces.

Regions with unusually high concentrations of magical energy are often heavily influenced by nearby Lay Lines. Over time, this influence can alter the geography, climate, flora, and fauna of an area, creating environments shaped by elemental energy.

In most cases, Lay Lines are not physical structures and cannot be directly touched or perceived. They permeate reality itself, existing more like an unseen layer woven into the fabric of the world. Most people are incapable of sensing them naturally, although certain magical beings, artifacts, and practitioners possess this ability.

Memories Within the Lay Lines

Beyond transporting magical energy, the Lay Lines are also capable of preserving echoes of the past. Powerful emotions, catastrophic events, prolonged magical activity, and even the presence of certain beings may leave impressions within the flow of magic.

Over centuries, these lingering traces accumulate like layers of sediment, forming what many scholars refer to as memory echoes. Through rituals, magic, or specialized abilities, it is possible to access fragments of these memories.

Elemental lifeforms are particularly known for interacting with these echoes. Some species are believed to feed upon the memories stored within the Lay Lines, using them to evolve, adapt, or inherit ancient instincts and knowledge.

The Lay Lines and the Afterlife

The Lay Lines are also deeply connected to the cycle of souls and the afterlife. According to most beliefs and magical studies, when living beings die, their souls eventually return to the Lay Lines and merge with their flow.

What happens afterward varies greatly. Some souls gradually dissolve over time, becoming one with the magical currents of the world, while others may be preserved or protected by higher beings such as gods, spirits, or other divine entities.

However, not all souls successfully integrate into the Lay Lines. Souls burdened by powerful emotions, unfinished desires, curses, or external interference may remain trapped between states of existence. These lost souls often become undead beings, wandering spirits, or other supernatural entities.

Lay Line Instability

Under normal circumstances, the Lay Lines remain in a relatively stable state, continuously transporting magical energy, souls, and memories throughout the world. However, certain events or phenomena may destabilize their flow, causing magical disturbances known as Lay Line Instabilities.

These instabilities may result in either a stagnation or an overflow of magical energy. Minor disturbances are relatively harmless and may only affect local wildlife or environmental conditions. More severe cases, however, can dramatically alter entire regions.

One of the most common consequences of unstable Lay Lines is the spontaneous creation of elemental lifeforms. Excess elemental energy may condense into living creatures, spirits, or other magical entities born directly from the imbalance itself.

In rare and extreme situations, the instability may also distort the climate, geography, and natural laws of a region. Forests may rapidly mutate, storms may persist for months, rivers may change direction, and entire ecosystems can become warped by uncontrolled magical influence.

The Moon of Madness

One of the most infamous phenomena associated with Lay Line instability is the Moon of Madness. During this event, the Lay Lines across the world become chaotic and unpredictable, causing widespread magical disturbances.

During the Moon of Madness, monsters become significantly more aggressive and appear in far greater numbers. Magical creatures often behave erratically, while humans and other intelligent races may suffer from illness, hallucinations, emotional instability, or magical corruption.

The environment itself is also affected. Sudden storms, unnatural temperatures, violent magical surges, and other unpredictable phenomena become increasingly common until the Lay Lines eventually stabilize once more.

Dragons and the Lay Lines

Dragons possess a uniquely destructive relationship with the Lay Lines. As they feed upon the magical energy flowing through them, the surrounding currents gradually begin to warp and reshape themselves according to the dragon's elemental nature.

Over time, this influence causes the region to produce increasing amounts of the element associated with the dragon. The climate, terrain, and ecosystem slowly transform as a result. A fire dragon may create volcanic regions and burning wastelands, while an ice dragon may gradually plunge entire territories into eternal winter.

The incubation period preceding a dragon's emergence is considered especially dangerous. During this phase, the surrounding Lay Lines often become highly unstable, resulting in unnatural weather, elemental disasters, magical mutations, and the appearance of abnormal creatures.

The Gods and the Lay Lines

The gods of the world possess a profound connection to the Lay Lines. Unlike mortal beings, they are not merely capable of interacting with the currents of magic — they have become part of them.

Long ago, during an ancient age of catastrophe, the Lay Lines were nearly destroyed by a malevolent higher being known as Nekros. His corruption spread through the magical currents of the world like a disease, threatening to unravel the balance of magic, souls, and reality itself.

To prevent the complete collapse of the world, the gods chose to sacrifice their permanent physical forms and merge themselves with the Lay Lines. Through this act, they were able to shield the magical currents from total corruption and eventually, with the aid of humanity and their heroes, defeat Nekros, sealing him away within a pocket real on the moon.

However, the victory was incomplete. Fragments of Nekros's essence remained scattered throughout the Lay Lines, where they continue to linger even in the present day. During periods of instability — especially during the Moon of Madness — these remnants become active once more, spreading corruption, provoking magical disasters, and driving creatures into violent frenzy.

In certain regions, larger concentrations of Nekros's essence condensed into entities known as Seeds of Ruin. These fragments are considered among the most dangerous phenomena associated with the Lay Lines.

Unlike ordinary corruption, Seeds of Ruin possess a form of pseudo-consciousness, behaving almost like incomplete extensions of Nekros himself. They are capable of warping the surrounding environment, corrupting living beings, creating monstrous lifeforms, and triggering catastrophic magical anomalies.

Scholars and priests consider them especially dangerous because their behavior is often unpredictable — even Nekros himself is believed to lack full control over them.

The Divine Domains

The merging of the gods with the Lay Lines fundamentally changed their existence. Although they lost their permanent physical bodies, they gained a far deeper connection to the world itself.

The gods are still capable of manifesting temporary avatars in the physical world, though doing so requires immense energy and is usually limited in duration. Their true existence now resides within the Lay Lines, where each deity maintains a spiritual domain connected to their nature and authority.

Through these domains, the gods are capable of influencing the regions under their protection. They may shape climates, strengthen natural cycles, bless chosen followers, stabilize damaged Lay Lines, or interfere with supernatural events occurring within their territory.

However, their existence is now intrinsically tied to the condition of the Lay Lines themselves. Damage, corruption, or instability within the magical currents can weaken the gods, limit their influence, or distort their connection to the mortal world.
