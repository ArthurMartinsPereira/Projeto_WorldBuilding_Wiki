The Lay Lines

The veins of magic that flow through the world.

What are the Lay Lines?

The Lay Lines are akin to circulatory system for magical energy, it contains, transports and distributes this energy across the world, helping to maintain the balance of magic, depending on the consentration of elemental energy in a region it may affect the geography, climate and even the fauna and flora that live there. The Lay Lines are also capable of more than just moving magic energy around, they are also capable of recording "memories" or information in a way, over time certain events and presences may leave behind echoes whithin the Lay Lines, these slowly settle into layers, like sediment, and they can be acessed through a variety of means. Another very important thing the Lay Lines are known for is its relation to the after-life, once living beings die their souls merge with the Lay Lines, from there many things can happen, some may slowly dissolve over time while others may be granted protection by higher beings like the gods. There are also those who are unable to fully integrate into the lay lines, these usualy become beings like the undead or vagrant sprits.
