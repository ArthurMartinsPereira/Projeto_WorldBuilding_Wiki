What is it and how it works.

What is Primal Magic?

Primal Magic is a term used to describe the use o magic in a non-structured, be it by people, animals, plants or monsters, when it comes to humans, those who specialize in the use of Primal Magic are refered to as Primalists, around 30% to 35% of the population is born with the ability to use Primal Magic, though most never try to truly master it. The use of Primal magic is tied to ones mind, body and soul, it's the most basic and instinctual form of using magic since it doesn't require much learning, anyone born capable of using magic is by defaut a primal magic user, though if they will specialize in the use of it or learn some different way of using magic, like Path Magic, is their decision. In most animals, plants and monsters that use magic, they are born with a special organ or body part that helps them channel their magic power, humanity on the other hand seem to lack these, instead it's like their whole bodies act as the conduit for their magic.
