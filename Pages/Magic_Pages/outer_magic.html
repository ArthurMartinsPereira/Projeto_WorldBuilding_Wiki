Outer Magic

Power that violates or surpasses the laws of magic.

What is Outer Magic?

The term Outer Magic is used to describe a form of magical power capable of bending, violating, or surpassing the natural laws of the world. This power is most commonly associated with Higher Beings such as the Gods and the Descenders, making it exceedingly rare for a mortal to wield it.

Scholars believe that Outer Magic originates from the realm beyond the mortal world—the place from which the Gods and other Descenders first arrived. Unlike conventional magic, Outer Magic is not bound by the structured laws that govern Primal Magic, Elemental Energy, or Path Magic.

Outer Magic has no universal set of abilities. Its effects vary greatly depending on its source, ranging from the manipulation of space and time to dominion over souls, matter, memories, or even the fundamental rules of reality. Each Higher Being may possesses a unique manifestation of this power, as long as they are powerful enough.

This diversity is believed to stem from its connection to the Arcane Element, the primordial source from which all forms of magic originated. While Arcane Energy serves as the foundation of magical power, Outer Magic represents an expression of that power unrestricted by the natural limitations imposed upon mortal spellcasting.

Accessing Outer Magic

Mortals cannot naturally wield Outer Magic through their own abilities. However, several known methods allow limited access to it.

  • Ousia: The divine essence and life force of Gods and Descenders. Exposure to concentrated Ousia may grant temporary or permanent access to Outer Magic.
  • Descender Remnants: Fragments left behind by Higher Beings that still contain traces of their power.
  • Ousia Bounds: A permanent bond formed between a mortal and a Higher Being. Through this connection, the mortal can draw upon the being's Outer Magic. The extent of this power depends entirely upon the Higher Being and the nature of the bond.

Hallowed Aberrations

On rare occasions, beasts, monsters, or other creatures may come into contact with Ousia or Descender Remnants. Most perish from the exposure, unable to withstand the overwhelming power.

Those that survive may undergo a profound transformation and become Hallowed Aberrations. These creatures gain the ability to wield forms of Outer Magic and often possess power comparable to that of Dragons. Unlike Dragons, however, Hallowed Aberrations are frequently unpredictable and enigmatic, displaying behaviors and motivations that are difficult for mortals to comprehend.
