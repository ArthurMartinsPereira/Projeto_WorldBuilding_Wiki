Outer Magic

Power that violates or surpasses the laws of magic.

What is Outer Magic?

Outer Magic is a form of magical power capable of violating, bending, or surpassing the natural laws that govern the world. Unlike conventional forms of magic, it is not bound by the same rules, limitations, or magical systems that regulate Primal Magic, Path Magic, or Elemental Power.

This power is most commonly associated with Higher Beings such as the Gods and Descenders, making it exceptionally rare among mortal races. Evidence suggests that Outer Magic originates from the dimension or reality from which these entities first emerged.

Nature of Outer Magic

Unlike regular magical systems, Outer Magic possesses no universal set of effects. Its capabilities vary greatly depending on the being wielding it. Known manifestations include the manipulation of space, time, matter, souls, and other fundamental aspects of existence.

Every Higher Being possesses its own unique variation of Outer Magic. This diversity is believed to stem from its connection to the Arcane Element, the primordial source from which all forms of magic originated. Because of this connection, Outer Magic is capable of expressing itself in ways that conventional magic cannot.

Outer Elements

Outer Magic often manifests through what scholars call Outer Elements, also known as Pseudo Elements. These are unique powers that do not belong to the natural elemental spectrum and instead represent distortions or extensions of reality itself.

While all Higher Beings possess some degree of Outer Magic, only the most powerful among them can develop truly unique Outer Elements and abilities. Lesser entities, such as the Nameless, typically wield simpler or more generic manifestations. Even these weaker forms, however, greatly surpass the capabilities of most Primal and Path Magic practitioners.

Limitations and Consequences

Despite its immense power, Outer Magic is not entirely free from consequences. Due to its alien nature, reality itself appears to resist or compensate for its influence. The greater the violation of natural law, the stronger this corrective response tends to be.

For mortal users, this often manifests as magical backlash, physical deterioration, mental strain, or other unforeseen side effects. Higher Beings are far less affected by these limitations due to their innate connection to the source of Outer Magic.

Mortals and Outer Magic

Mortals are incapable of naturally accessing Outer Magic. However, several known methods allow them to temporarily or permanently draw upon its power.

Ousia

Ousia, the life force and essence of Gods and Descenders, can be used in rituals, magical experiments, and the creation of powerful artifacts. Such practices are rare and often dangerous due to the unstable nature of the power involved.

Descender Remnants

Fragments, remains, or residual traces left behind by Gods and Descenders retain portions of their Ousia. These remnants are highly sought after by scholars, cults, and powerful organizations due to their potential applications.

Ousia Bounds

In exceptionally rare cases, a God or Descender may grant a portion of its essence directly to a mortal. When this occurs, a permanent connection known as an Ousia Bound is formed.

This bond creates a two-way link between the mortal and the Higher Being, allowing the recipient to access a portion of that entity's Outer Magic. The connected Higher Being can usually regulate how much power is made available, though the exact degree of control varies from case to case.

Inheritance

Though most Ousia Bounds are originally granted directly by a God or Descender, the bond can occasionally be passed down through future generations. Descendants of an Ousia Bound may inherit traces of the original connection, carrying fragments of the Higher Being's essence within their bloodline.

The strength of this inheritance usually weakens with each successive generation. If the power remains unused or unawakened for long periods, the bond may enter a dormant state and eventually fade entirely.

Dormant Ousia Bounds can sometimes awaken under extreme circumstances, particularly during moments of intense emotional distress, mortal danger, or near-death experiences. Such awakenings are often sudden and difficult to control, occasionally revealing powers that had remained hidden within a family for centuries.

Because of their hereditary nature, Ousia Bounds are often referred to by alternative names such as Old Blood, Divine Blood, or Blessed Lineages, depending on the culture and region.

Hallowed Aberrations

On rare occasions, monsters or other creatures may absorb remnants of a God or Descender. Most perish during the process, unable to withstand the transformation.

Those that survive and successfully adapt become beings known as Hallowed Aberrations. These creatures gain access to Outer Magic and possess power comparable to that of Dragons. However, they are often far more mysterious, isolated, and poorly understood than their draconic counterparts.
