The Ten Elements

Representing the different ways magic energy is manifested in the world

Overview

In nature magic rarely remains in its purest state, most of the time this volatile energy ends up splitting into various more stable forms, these are the elements we know. In total there are 10 elements, 3 of them are classified as the Higher Elements, while the other 7 are referred to as the Lesser/Natural Elements. Magic energy itself can be divided into two main kinds, Positive and Negative, the positive spectrum represents the imaterial while the negative represents the material, the 10 elements possess varying quantities of each of these kinds.

The Lesser Elements

Also known as the Natural Elements, these 7 are the most common ones to be found in nature. Animus, Electro and Ignis belong to the positive elemental spectrum, while Geo, Cryo and Aqua belong to the negative, Zephyr pocesses equal amounts of negative and positive energy, being a more neutral element.

  • Animus
  • Electro
  • Ignis
  • Zephyr
  • Aqua
  • Cryo
  • Geo

The Higher Elements

These elements are much rarer to be observed in nature, and much powerful and dangerous to handle. Light represents pure positive energy while darkness represents pure negative energy, the Arcane represents magic in its purest and rawest form, being the Source Element, all others are aspects of it, the Arcane is composed of a perfectly balanced blend of positive and negative energies.

  • Arcane
  • Light (Lumini)
  • Darkness (Umbral)
