The Ten Elements

Representing the different ways magic energy manifests in the world.

Overview

In nature magic rarely remains in its purest state, most of the time this volatile energy ends up splitting into various more stable forms, these are the elements we know. In total there are 10 elements, 3 of them are classified as the Higher Elements, while the other 7 are referred to as the Lesser/Natural Elements.

Magic energy itself can be divided into two main kinds, Positive and Negative, the positive spectrum represents the immaterial while the negative represents the material, the 10 elements possess varying quantities of each of these kinds.

Across the world elemental energy flows through structures known as the Lay Lines, these are not phisical but more akin to a giant web or root system that flows through the world, they contain and transport the elemental energy to different places and in the process shapping the geography and climate of different regions.

The Lesser Elements

Also known as the Natural Elements, these 7 are the most common ones to be found in nature. Animus, Electro and Ignis belong to the positive elemental spectrum, while Geo, Cryo and Aqua belong to the negative, Zephyr possesses equal amounts of negative and positive energy, being a more neutral element.

  • Animus
  • Electro
  • Ignis
  • Zephyr
  • Aqua
  • Cryo
  • Geo

The Higher Elements

These elements are much rarer to be observed in nature, and much more powerful and dangerous to handle. Light represents pure positive energy while darkness represents pure negative energy, the Arcane represents magic in its purest and rawest form, being the Source Element, all others are aspects of it, the Arcane is composed of a perfectly balanced blend of positive and negative energies.

  • Arcane
  • Light (Luminis)
  • Darkness (Umbral)

Elemental Affinity

Every being that can use magic is attuned to at least one of the 10 elements, it's possible to have more than one affinity, but especially for humans, the more elements one has the higher the stress on their bodies, this can cause people to have a more fragile health, weaker physique and even shorter lifespans on the worst cases. For humans, having up to two elemental affinities is the safe limit, some people can also occasionally be born with a partial attunement to other elements, allowing them to manipulate them but not generate the element themselves, these individuals are usulay called Conduits or Resonators, on their case they don't suffer negative side effects for having control of other elements, this is due to their inability to generate the elements by themselves. These individuals also lack the benefits of these extra elements since they aren't fully attined to them.
